## Why Assault Rifles Still Dominate the Meta  
  
Snipers are great at distance. SMGs shine up close. But assault rifles live in the messy middle — the 30 to 200 meter range where most real firefights actually happen in Classic and Ranked modes. They forgive positioning mistakes, work with almost any attachment setup, and give you flexibility you simply don't get from a single-purpose weapon.  
  
That's why every serious player, from Platinum to Conqueror, runs at least one AR in their loadout.

If PUBG Mobile had a mascot weapon, it would be the M416. It has been relevant in literally every season since launch, and 2026 is no different.  
  
What keeps it on top isn't raw damage — the M416 hits for 41 base damage per bullet, which is average at best. What keeps it on top is *control*. The recoil pattern on the M416 is consistent and learnable. Once you understand how it pulls, you can compensate almost perfectly, especially if you're running a 4x or 6x scope at distance.  
  
With a full attachment setup (compensator, vertical foregrip, extended quick-draw mag, half-grip or thumb grip), the M416 turns into a laser beam. Tap firing at range? Consistent. Spraying at 50 meters? Manageable. That predictability under pressure is exactly why this gun holds S-tier year after year.  
  
**Best With:** 3x/6x for mid-range, 2x for close-mid. Pair with a sniper or DMR for complete map coverage.

### Beryl M762 — High Risk, Massive Reward  
  
The Beryl is a beast that punishes people who don't respect it — including the person holding it.  
  
It fires 7.62mm rounds, which means 47 base damage per shot. At that rate, the time-to-kill is noticeably shorter than the M416, which matters enormously in close-quarters. A Beryl user who lands their shots in a building fight or a 30-meter sprint push is going to win that exchange almost every time.  
  
The catch? Recoil. The M762 bucks hard, especially on full auto, and the horizontal sway is less predictable than the M416's. This isn't a beginner gun. If you haven't put in the time to learn its pattern, you'll dump a full magazine and miss half of it while your target one-taps you back.  
  
However — once you tame it? The Beryl is arguably the more dangerous gun of the two. Especially on maps like Sanhok or in late-circle building fights where engagements are punchy and fast.  
  
**Best With:** Vertical foregrip or half-grip to manage the kick. Compensator is mandatory. Run a 2x or 3x for best results.  
  
| Stat | Value |  
|------|-------|  
| Base Damage | 47 |  
| Magazine (base) | 30 |  
| Fire Rate | 0.0857s |  
| Range | Short–Medium |  
| Ammo Type | 7.62mm |  
| Attachment Slots | 5 |  
  
**Verdict:** Second gun pick for skilled players. Devastating in close-mid range. Don't run it on full auto at long range unless you enjoy wasted bullets.

## A-Tier ARs — Genuinely Powerful, Slight Drawbacks  
  
### AKM — The Loot-Spawn Beast  
  
The AKM is what you grab when there's nothing better on the floor, and then you realize halfway through the round that you don't actually need anything better.  
  
It deals 47 damage per bullet — same as the Beryl — but the fire rate is slightly slower, which paradoxically makes it a bit more manageable in controlled bursts. The AKM lacks a grip slot, which is its biggest weakness in the current meta. You can put a muzzle and stock on it, but without a foregrip, taming the vertical recoil on full auto is entirely on your muscle memory and thumb control.  
  
What it does exceptionally well: punching through level 2 armor fast. At range, burst-fire AKM is more accurate than most players expect. It's a gun that rewards discipline over spray.  
  
| Stat | Value |  
|------|-------|  
| Base Damage | 47 |  
| Magazine (base) | 30 |  
| Fire Rate | 0.100s |  
| Range | Short–Medium |  
| Ammo Type | 7.62mm |  
| Attachment Slots | 3 |  
  
**Verdict:** Strong early-game and reliable mid-game. The grip-slot limitation keeps it out of S-tier.

### SCAR-L — The Underrated Workhorse  
  
The SCAR-L gets overlooked because the M416 exists, and that's genuinely unfair. The base damage and fire rate are nearly identical, and the SCAR-L actually has *lower* recoil stock than the M416. Before attachments, the SCAR-L is easier to control.  
  
Where it falls short is the max-potential ceiling. Fully kitted, the M416 edges ahead in overall controllability. But if you're in a loot-poor drop or playing an aggressive style where you fire first before fully setting up? The SCAR-L is more forgiving out of the box.

| Fire Rate | 0.0962s |  
| Range | Medium |  
| Ammo Type | 5.56mm |  
| Attachment Slots | 4 |  
  
**Verdict:** Solid second-best 5.56 option. Pick it confidently in the early game.

### Groza — Airdrop-Only, Absolutely Oppressive  
  
The Groza only comes from care packages, so most rounds you won't see it. But when you do — or when you take it off a body — it changes the dynamic of every fight you're in.  
  
It fires 7.62mm rounds with a fire rate that's genuinely faster than the AKM, and the damage output per second is among the highest of any AR. Up close, it's practically an SMG in terms of how fast it deletes enemies. The downside is limited attachments and the fact that ammo burns through fast when you're spraying at that rate.  
  
The Groza doesn't need a tier explanation. It's an airdrop weapon for a reason.  
  
| Stat | Value |  
|------|-------|  
| Base Damage | 47 |  
| Magazine (base) | 30 |  
| Fire Rate | 0.080s |  
| Range | Short–Medium |  
| Ammo Type | 7.62mm |  
| Attachment Slots | 2 |  
  
**Verdict:** If it drops from the sky and you're in position, fight for it. It wins fights the M416 can't.

## B-Tier ARs — Situationally Decent  
  
### QBZ95  
  
Available only in Sanhok, the QBZ95 is a solid alternative to the SCAR-L on that map. It's smooth, controllable, and spawns commonly enough that you'll find it with some attachments already. Not a powerhouse, but absolutely functional.  
  
### G36C  
  
Vikendi exclusive. Similar role to the QBZ95 — a map-specific 5.56 option that won't let you down but won't wow you either. Works best in mid-range engagements where you can pace your shots.

## C-Tier ARs — Use Only If Necessary  
  
### M16A4  
  
Single-fire and burst only. In a game where full-auto is the norm, being locked to burst puts you at a significant disadvantage in reactive fights. Experienced players can tap this effectively at long range, but that same role is better filled by a DMR.  
  
### AUG A3  
  
Airdrop exclusive, which used to make it feel special. In 2026 the meta has caught up and other airdrop weapons (Groza, DBS) offer more consistent value. Not terrible, but not worth prioritizing.

## How to Pick the Right AR for Your Playstyle  
  
**Aggressive Rusher:** Beryl M762 or Groza. You want maximum damage in close-quarters, and you're willing to master the recoil.  
  
**Passive/Sniper Support:** M416. Use it for mid-range control while your sniper handles distance. The consistency is the point.  
  
**Ranked Grinder:** M416 every time. In ranked, you need a gun you can trust in high-pressure moments. Its predictable recoil pattern makes it one of the most consistent rifles to rely on when the circle is closing and mistakes cost points.  
  
**New or Intermediate Player:** SCAR-L or M416. Both are forgiving and teach you proper burst-fire discipline without punishing every mistake.

## My Personal Take — After Hundreds of Classic Matches  
  
After grinding Classic and Ranked through multiple seasons, my go-to is still the M416. Not because it's flashy — it isn't — but because it's the rifle I trust most when a fight breaks out unexpectedly. You know exactly what it's going to do when you pull the trigger, and in high-pressure moments that predictability is worth more than raw damage numbers on paper.  
  
That said, if you've put serious time into recoil control and you're comfortable on gyroscope, the Beryl rewards that investment hard. There have been rounds where I've picked up a kitted Beryl off a body and ended up with more kills than any M416 game I've played — it just hits different when you're landing every shot in a building fight.  
  
My honest recommendation: grind your sensitivity and gyro settings with the M416 until your recoil control is solid, then experiment with the Beryl as a second option. The muscle memory transfers, and you'll understand exactly why the Beryl's kick feels harder to tame by comparison.

## FAQ  
  
**Q: Is the M416 still the best AR in PUBG Mobile in 2026?**  
Yes. The M416 continues to hold the top spot because of its versatility, manageable recoil, and five attachment slots. It's the most reliable assault rifle across all ranges, maps, and skill levels. Unless you're an experienced player specifically targeting a close-range meta, nothing outperforms it as an all-round AR.  
  
**Q: Is the Beryl M762 better than the AKM?**  
In most situations, yes. The Beryl has the same damage but a faster fire rate, which means a higher DPS ceiling. The key difference is the grip slot — the Beryl accepts a foregrip, making it significantly more controllable with the right setup. If both are fully kitted, the Beryl wins. If neither has attachments, they're roughly equal.  
  
**Q: What's the best AR for close-range fights?**  
The Beryl M762 or Groza if you can get it. Both fire 7.62mm rounds at high rates, which means enemies go down fast in building fights and squad wipes. The Groza is technically superior but is airdrop-only.  
  
**Q: Should beginners use the AKM or M416?**  
M416, without question. The AKM's lack of a grip slot means its recoil is harder to control without practice, and the 7.62mm recoil is more aggressive in general. The M416 teaches you good habits and punishes mistakes less harshly while you're learning.  
  
**Q: Does the QBZ95 compete with the M416?**  
Not quite, but it's closer than most people think. On Sanhok, where it spawns, the QBZ95 is a genuinely solid choice with smooth recoil and decent fire rate. If you can't find an M416, don't sleep on the QBZ95 — it'll carry you through most fights.  
  
**Q: What's the best scope for assault rifles?**  
It depends on your range. A 3x scope is the most versatile option and works well from 30 to 150 meters. A 6x on an M416 is excellent for tap-firing at long range. Avoid putting a 6x or 8x on a Beryl or AKM — the recoil makes it impractical.  
  
**Q: Is the SCAR-L worth using over the M416?**  
If you find a SCAR-L early and an M416 later, swap. But don't go out of your way to replace a kitted SCAR-L mid-game — it's a capable gun. The difference between a fully attached M416 and a fully attached SCAR-L is noticeable but not game-changing at most skill levels.
