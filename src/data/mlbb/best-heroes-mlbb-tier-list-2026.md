## Post-2.1.67 Meta Context

Before the role-by-role breakdown, here's the patch landscape these rankings sit inside:

**Patch 2.1.67 (April 22, 2026):** Delivered a sweeping Gold Lane marksman overhaul — mana costs, base movement speed, and durability were adjusted across most of the role. Early-game laners lost some natural bulk; late-game mobile carries saw durability growth reduced. Aulus was also revamped with a new kit in this patch.

**Patch 2.1.67a (May 14, 2026):** The follow-up hotfix cut Harley's mid-game burst, buffed Chou and Paquito, and began a pass on roaming tank survivability.

The result: faster mid-game objective tempo, a more volatile Gold Lane, and a shift toward fighters and roamers who create pressure across all three stages of the game rather than one.

## Tank Tier List

The current tank meta rewards sustain and crowd-control initiation over pure stat-checking. Gloo received adjustments in Patch 2.1.61 but his core kit still creates disruption in ranked environments. Kaja earns his place through objective-deciding suppress potential rather than raw durability.

| Tier | Heroes |
|------|--------|
| **S** | Gloo, Kaja |
| **A** | Tigreal, Hylos, Baxia, Lolita |
| **B** | Edith, Gatotkaca, Esmeralda, Barats, Belerick |
| **C** | Atlas, Khufra |

**Gloo** — his kit lets him split and attach to an enemy hero, transferring incoming damage to that target. This creates problems for burst heroes who need to land a full combo cleanly to kill. He's disruptive to lane, to objectives, and to dive patterns that expect a straightforward kill window.

**Kaja** — classified as Fighter/Support but commonly played as a frontline roamer. His suppress ultimate locks a single target — usually the enemy carry — long enough for your full team to burst them. In objective fights around Turtle or Lord, that one suppress is often the swing play.

**Weak against:** Heroes with sustained damage that bypasses his split mechanic (Kimmy, Hanabi). Kaja is countered by compositions that don't cluster a single priority target.

## Fighter / EXP Lane Tier List

Season 40 EXP lane favors fighters with contribution across all three game phases — laning, skirmishes, and 5v5s. Heroes that spike in one phase and fall off later have trended downward in community meta discussions.

| Tier | Heroes |
|------|--------|
| **S** | Chou, Sun, Julian |
| **A** | Paquito, Thamuz, Esmeralda, Terizla |
| **B** | Guinevere, Jawhead, Ruby |
| **C** | Zilong, Alucard, Silvanna |

**Chou** — buffed in the 2.1.67a hotfix, reinforcing an already reliable kit. His CC chain into kick displacement allows clean pickoffs on overextended enemies, and his defensive dash gives him survivability tools that most fighters lack.

**Julian** — his skill chaining allows sequences that shift between physical and magical damage without a cooldown gap, making him difficult to itemise against. Strong when executed well; has a higher skill floor than most EXP laners.

**Sun** — clone mechanics apply split pressure and create confusion in teamfights. His effectiveness comes from forcing opponents to misidentify the real Sun during objective skirmishes.

> **Note:** Julian's S-tier placement reflects community meta discussion. In Epic and below, Paquito or Chou tend to deliver more consistent results due to a lower execution requirement.

**Weak against:** Chou struggles vs heroes with long-range poke that punish his approach windows. Julian is countered by tank-heavy comps that absorb his burst before it reaches carries.

## Assassin / Jungle Tier List

Post-2.1.67a jungle meta favors mobility, fast camp clear, and the ability to convert early gold leads into map pressure. The strongest picks either carry burst-and-escape kits or provide cross-map objective influence.

| Tier | Heroes |
|------|--------|
| **S** | Gusion, Hayabusa, Harley |
| **A** | Ling, Lancelot, Yi Sun-shin, Benedetta, Karina |
| **B** | Fanny, Saber, Selena |
| **C** | Natalia, Hanzo, Roger |

**Gusion** — high-ceiling burst assassin with a rotation that eliminates priority targets before they can react. His mobility lets him disengage from failed attempts more cleanly than most assassins. Strong snowball potential when ahead in solo queue.

**Hayabusa** — consistent Mythic-level pick with reliable mobility and single-target burst. Less reliant on mechanical perfection than Gusion; a better fit for players who want a high-performing jungler with a manageable skill curve.

**Yi Sun-shin** (A-tier) — global ultimate creates cross-map pressure that shapes how teams approach Turtle and Lord, but converting that pressure into wins requires coordination. Strong in premade or semi-coordinated play; less consistent in solo queue.

> **On Fanny:** Mechanically she's one of the highest-ceiling junglers in the game. She's placed in B-tier here because her floor is punishing — an average Fanny loses games; a skilled Fanny wins them. Learn her in unranked before taking her into competitive matches.

**Weak against:** Gusion is countered by hard CC and tank-heavy compositions that absorb his burst. Hayabusa falls off against heroes with sustained damage and CC that prevents his shadow reactivation.

## Mage / Mid Lane Tier List

Mid lane priority in Season 40 is wave clear speed, rotation ability, and teamfight zone control. Heroes that clear fast, move around the map efficiently, and threaten from range create objective advantages even without mechanical outplays.

| Tier | Heroes |
|------|--------|
| **S** | Zhuxin, Luo Yi |
| **A** | Pharsa, Valentina, Kagura, Vale, Harith |
| **B** | Cyclops, Aurora, Cecilion, Lylia |
| **C** | Nana, Vexana, Odette |

**Zhuxin** — Skill 1 displacement is difficult to play around in lane. Opponents who don't respect her range get punished by repeated knockbacks that break positioning before a fight even starts.

**Luo Yi** — portal mechanic changes team rotation patterns. At higher elo, her ability to teleport allies mid-fight creates repositioning options that opponents often can't adjust to in real time.

**Pharsa** (A-tier) — received nerfs in Patch 2.1.61 which dropped her from S-tier consideration, but her movement transformation (Wings by Wings) still gives her rotation speed most mages lack. Ultimate remains useful for zoning objective edges.

> **Harith note:** Listed A-tier for mid but functions as a flex pick — he can run as a magic-focused Gold Lane hero in drafts that need balanced damage types.

**Weak against:** Zhuxin is countered by heroes with dashes or gap-closers that negate her displacement value. Luo Yi's portal is limited by range — in chaotic teamfights the setup window can be disrupted.

## Marksman / Gold Lane Tier List

Patch 2.1.67's Gold Lane overhaul made the lane more volatile. Strong early laners lost durability bulk; late-game mobile carries had growth reduced. The lane now rewards cleaner positioning and punishes passive play harder than before.

| Tier | Heroes |
|------|--------|
| **S** | Hanabi, Kimmy |
| **A** | Granger, Melissa, Beatrix |
| **B** | Moskov, Brody, Miya |
| **C** | Layla, Clint, Bruno |

**Hanabi** — passive bouncing attacks scale with how clustered the enemy team is. In the current Lord-centric meta where teams naturally group, she creates disproportionate damage. Her bounce chain also applies slows, making her teamfight threat significantly higher than her laning phase suggests. That teamfight ceiling is why she sits above Granger, whose value concentrates in laning and pick-off scenarios.

**Melissa** — doll mechanic punishes dive-heavy compositions. Any assassin or fighter who jumps onto her triggers the doll's knockback, buying enough time for her team to respond. Useful in ranked environments where dive-heavy comps are common at Legend and above.

**Weak against:** Hanabi is countered by dive assassins (Lancelot, Gusion) who can burst her before bounces proc. Melissa's doll has limited effectiveness vs ranged heroes who poke from outside its range.

## Support / Roam Tier List

Support impact in Season 40 is measured by healing uptime, how well a hero can peel for their carry, and whether they create win conditions without needing resources. The best roam picks this season either provide healing that scales globally or CC that counters the dominant team compositions.

| Tier | Heroes |
|------|--------|
| **S** | Floryn, Diggie |
| **A** | Angela, Mathilda, Rafaela, Estes |
| **B** | Faramis, Carmilla, Nana (support build) |
| **C** | Minotaur, Lolita (non-tank build) |

**Floryn** — her ultimate (Bloom) heals all allies globally twice, regardless of where they are on the map. Her passive (Dew's Lantern) lets her share the Flower of Hope with one teammate, granting that ally a shield each time Floryn heals them. Community tracking sites report her win rate consistently above 50% in Season 40 ranked play, making her one of the more statistically reliable support picks this season.

**Diggie** — his ultimate removes all crowd-control effects from nearby teammates during its duration. In a meta where CC-heavy compositions are common at Legend and above, that CC immunity window turns losing teamfights into survivable engages.

> **Marcel note:** Classified Support/Tank and commonly used in the Roam lane, but placed in the Overall S-Tier section above because his impact is primarily a draft consideration at high ranks. When available at lower brackets, he's a strong teamfight-disruptor pick worth learning.

**Weak against:** Floryn is hard-countered by Baxia — his passive reduces shield and HP regen effects on any hero he hits with a skill, directly suppressing her healing output. Diggie's ultimate has a delay before activating, which skilled opponents can exploit.

## Best Heroes for Beginners in 2026

New to MLBB or returning after a long break? These heroes have kits that don't punish mechanical errors as harshly as high-mobility carries, making them better choices while you're still building map awareness and game sense.

| Hero | Role | Why They're Beginner-Friendly |
|------|------|-------------------------------|
| **Tigreal** | Tank | His ultimate pulls and stuns all nearby enemies — simple to land, high-impact, forgiving of imperfect positioning |
| **Miya** | Marksman | Pure basic-attack hero; teaches you damage timing and kiting without complex skill sequences |
| **Estes** | Support | Channel heal and passive healing mean you contribute without needing mechanical precision |
| **Layla** | Marksman | Longest base attack range in the game; great for learning positioning fundamentals from a safe distance |
| **Balmond** | Fighter | High HP, area-of-effect skills, forgiving cooldowns — good for learning EXP lane matchup dynamics |

Hold off on Fanny, Gusion, and Ling until you have experience. Their floors are punishing — mistakes cost games, not just deaths.

**Q: Which hero is most effective for climbing solo queue right now?**

## Final Thoughts

The Season 40 meta rewards heroes that contribute at multiple stages of the game — not just one strong phase. A well-played A-tier pick almost always outperforms a poorly executed S-tier one, so start with what you can pilot confidently and build from there.

**Quick role reference:** Floryn or Diggie for roam. Chou or Julian for EXP lane. Gusion or Hayabusa for jungle. Zhuxin or Luo Yi for mid lane. Hanabi or Melissa for Gold lane. Gloo or Kaja for tank.
