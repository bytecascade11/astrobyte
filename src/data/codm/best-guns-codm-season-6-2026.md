## Overview 

Season 6 "Take Your Heart" dropped on July 1, and along with the Persona 5 Royal crossover, it quietly reshuffled which guns are actually worth grinding for right now. I've spent the past two weeks running these picks through Ranked, Multiplayer, and Battle Royale lobbies to see what actually holds up outside of tier-list theorycrafting. Here's what's working.

## The Season 6 Meta at a Glance

The headline addition this season is the **FSS Hurricane**, a new SMG that ships with the Take Your Heart Battle Pass. It trades some of the usual SMG fragility for a bigger magazine and noticeably tighter mid-range spread, which makes it playable outside the close-quarters box most SMGs get stuck in.

Outside the new arrival, the guns that were already carrying lobbies before July 1 haven't gone anywhere. Balance changes this cycle were minor, so most of the pre-season top tier held its ground.

## Best Assault Rifle: RAM-7 (and Why It Beats DR-H and BP50)

The RAM-7 is the AR tied to Violet, the Royal-exclusive operator in this collaboration, but it earns the top spot on its own merits. In my testing, the difference between the RAM-7 and guns like the DR-H or BP50 comes down to recoil recovery: the RAM-7's pattern resets faster between bursts, which matters more than raw damage in the back-and-forth peeking that Ranked lobbies are full of right now. The DR-H is still a fine beginner AR — its recoil is easier to predict even if it's not as fast — but once you've put in a few hundred kills of muscle memory, the RAM-7 pulls ahead.

**Gunsmith build I'm running:** OWC Marksman barrel for range, Fully Loaded stock for reduced flinch, and a Tac Laser to tighten hip-fire for close encounters. Pair it with the Hardline perk if you're pushing objectives, since faster streak charges matter more with an AR that can hold multiple lanes.

## Best SMG: QQ9 vs. FSS Hurricane vs. PP19 Bizon

This is where player type matters most. In my hands, the QQ9 still wins the majority of point-blank duels — its time-to-kill inside 10 meters is the fastest of the three, but it demands tight recoil control to hit that ceiling.

The FSS Hurricane is the better pick if you're not confident in your close-range aim yet. It doesn't out-duel the QQ9 up close, but its extra ammo capacity and flatter recoil pattern mean it forgives missed shots in a way the QQ9 doesn't — that's genuinely useful in Battle Royale, where fights often stretch past the first three bullets.

The PP19 Bizon sits between the two: it loses to the FSS Hurricane on magazine size and to the QQ9 on raw speed, but its recoil is the easiest of the three to control on a touchscreen without gyro aim. If you're playing without gyro or a controller, the Bizon is the more consistent choice; controller and gyro players will get more out of the QQ9's higher skill ceiling.

**Gunsmith build for the QQ9:** OWC Laser — Tactical for ADS speed, a light barrel for handling, and the Fastback 17 or similar quickdraw grip. Run it with the Gunfighter Wildcard so you can stack five attachments instead of the usual three.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the FSS Hurricane worth grinding for in Season 6?**
Yes, if you play Battle Royale or objective modes where longer engagements are common. It's less essential for players who only play close-quarters Multiplayer, where the QQ9 still edges it out.

**What's the best free-to-play gun in Season 6?**
The QQ9 and RAM-7 are both obtainable through normal account leveling and Weapon XP progression, making them the most accessible strong picks without needing Battle Pass tiers.

**Did Season 6 nerf or buff any snipers?**
No sniper-specific changes shipped with the Take Your Heart update. The Locus and Type 63 both carry over their pre-season standing unchanged.

**Is the SO-14 still viable in Season 6?**
It's earnable as a Ranked reward blueprint this season, which suggests it's still considered competitively relevant, though it remains a high-skill weapon that punishes inconsistent aim.

**Which gun is better for touchscreen players without gyro?**
The PP19 Bizon and Holger 26 both have flatter, more predictable recoil patterns, making them easier to control without gyro aiming than the QQ9 or RAM-7.

## Which Gun Should You Actually Pick?

If you want one straightforward recommendation instead of a full ranking, here's how I'd split it by player type:

- **Beginners:** FSS Hurricane or PP19 Bizon — both forgive missed shots and don't punish imperfect recoil control.
- **Ranked grinders:** QQ9 for close-range lobbies, RAM-7 if your maps lean more mid-range.
- **Aggressive rushers:** QQ9 paired with the aggressive sniper build above as a backup for cross-map picks.
- **Battle Royale:** Holger 26 as your primary, KRM-262 as a close-range panic button when you get caught looting.

Season 6's gun meta is more evolution than revolution — the FSS Hurricane is a genuinely useful addition, but the guns that were winning lobbies in June are still winning them now. Pick the build that matches how you actually play, put in the Weapon XP, and you'll be set for both Ranked and the new Persona 5 Royal-themed Plunder mode without needing to relearn your loadouts from scratch.
