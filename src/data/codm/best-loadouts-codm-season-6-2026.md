## Overview 

Picking a "best gun" is only half the job. Season 6, Take Your Heart, arrived on July 1 with the Persona 5 Royal crossover and one genuinely new option in the FSS Hurricane. The guns that dominated Ranked lobbies in June remain among the strongest choices, though the Hurricane has shifted some close-range matchups — it doesn't out-duel the QQ9 head-on, but its bigger magazine changes how those fights play out when the first three bullets miss. Below are five loadouts built around what actually matters this patch: close-range consistency for the Hurricane and QQ9, mid-range control for the DR-H, sustained fire for Battle Royale with the Holger 26, and a fast-ADS sniper build for Locus users still chasing one-shots.

Each build lists the five Gunsmith attachment slots plus the Red/Green/Blue perk row separately, since perks sit outside the attachment slots in-game. Trade-offs and swap options are noted under every table so you can adjust for your own playstyle instead of copying blind.

## FSS Hurricane — All-Rounder SMG Build

The Hurricane trades a bit of raw stopping power for a bigger magazine and flatter spread past 10 meters, which makes it easier to play from behind cover instead of purely run-and-gun.

| Slot | Attachment | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Muzzle | Monolithic Suppressor | Hides you off the minimap and adds range, but slows ADS speed slightly |
| Barrel | OWC Light Barrel | Faster handling and sprint-out, at the cost of a bit of damage range |
| Underbarrel | Ranger Foregrip | Tames vertical recoil during sustained bursts, small hit to ADS speed |
| Ammunition | Extended Mag B | Buys extra bullets for trade fights, slower reload |
| Rear Grip | Stippled Grip Tape | Tighter hip-fire spread, no meaningful downside |

**Perks:** Lightweight (Red) for movement speed, Toughness (Green) to cut flinch when you're hit mid-fight, Fully Loaded (Blue) to spawn with a topped-off reserve.

*Alternative:* if you'd rather stay off the radar completely, swap the Monolithic Suppressor for a lighter suppressor and accept the smaller range bonus — useful in Ranked where UAV spam is constant.

## QQ9 — Aggressive Close-Range Build

| Slot | Attachment | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Muzzle | OWC Light Compensator | Faster ADS speed, slightly reduced effective range |
| Barrel | Light Barrel | Improves sprint-to-fire and strafe speed, minor recoil increase |

## DR-H — Mid-Range Assault Rifle Build

For lobbies where fights open up past SMG range, the DR-H remains the steadier pick over spray-heavy ARs, especially on maps with longer sightlines.

| Slot | Attachment | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Muzzle | Monolithic Suppressor | Suppresses sound and adds range, small ADS speed penalty |
| Barrel | OWC Marksman | Extends effective damage range and tightens recoil, minor mobility loss |
| Stock | No Stock | Faster movement speed while ADS, slightly less stability |
| Ammunition | 25 Round OTM Mag | Faster ADS time over the stock magazine, no real downside |
| Rear Grip | Stippled Grip Tape | Steadier aim on sustained bursts |

**Perks:** Lightweight (Red), Toughness (Green) to reduce flinch at range, Dead Silence (Blue) to mask footsteps while repositioning.

*Alternative:* if you prefer holding sightlines over rotating, replace the Stock slot with MIP Strike Stock instead of No Stock — you'll trade some ADS speed for noticeably better stability while stationary.

## Holger 26 — Battle Royale LMG Build

Battle Royale in Isolated rewards ammo capacity over pure TTK, and that's before factoring in the new Plunder Treasure Hunt mode, which pulls fights into tighter zones around the Persona 5 Royal domains. The Holger 26 holds a lane better than almost anything else in the class — where the Locus wins a single opening pick, the Holger keeps firing through a full squad push without a reload, which matters more once a fight in a Plunder domain turns into a 3-on-1.

| Slot | Attachment | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Muzzle | Monolithic Suppressor | Stay hidden while holding a zone, minor ADS speed penalty |
| Barrel | OWC Marksman | Adds range without hurting mobility too much, small recoil increase |
| Underbarrel | Ranger Foregrip | Keeps sustained fire on target, slightly slower ADS |
| Ammunition | Fast Mag | Cuts reload downtime during rotations, no meaningful downside |
| Rear Grip | Granulated Grip Tape | Improves hip-fire accuracy for close ambushes |

**Perks:** Persistence (Red) to keep scorestreak progress on death, Quick Fix (Green) for instant health regen after a kill, Dexterity (Blue) for faster mounting and weapon-swap speed.

*Alternative:* if you'd rather sacrifice reload speed for more raw magazine size, swap Fast Mag for an Extended Mag — better for holding a single position, worse for rotating between zones.

## Locus — One-Shot Sniper Build

Quickscopers who haven't switched off Locus this season don't need to. Fast ADS combined with a reliable one-shot range still makes it the safer pick over the higher-skill-floor Type 63.

| Slot | Attachment | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Barrel | Light Barrel | Improves handling and sprint-out, small drop in bullet velocity |
| Laser | OWC Laser - Tactical | Faster scope-in speed, but the visible laser gives away your position up close |
| Optic | Variable Zoom Scope | Flexibility between close and long peeks, less stable at max zoom |
| Rear Grip | Granulated Grip Tape | Steadier aim while scoped in |
| Stock | No Stock | Faster ADS speed for quickscoping, less stability if you hold the scope |

**Perks:** Alert (Blue) for footstep warnings while scoped, Airborne (Green) for better in-air accuracy on Battle Royale fights, Lightweight (Red) for faster repositioning between shots.

*Alternative:* for a hold-angles playstyle rather than quickscoping, swap No Stock for MIP Strike Stock and the Light Barrel for OWC Marksman — you'll lose ADS speed but gain one-shot range and stability.

## FAQ

**Is the FSS Hurricane worth grinding the Battle Pass for?**
If you play a lot of Battle Royale or trade-heavy Ranked matches, yes — the extra magazine size covers for missed shots better than most SMGs in the class.

**Do attachments and perks compete for the same slots?**
No — attachments (up to 5 per weapon) and perks (Red, Green, Blue) live in separate menus in CODM's Gunsmith. You never have to trade one for the other.

**Has the meta shifted much since Season 5?**
Not dramatically. Take Your Heart added one meta-relevant weapon in the FSS Hurricane; the guns that were strong in June are still strong now.

**Which loadout is best for a beginner?**
The FSS Hurricane build is the most forgiving of the five — its recoil pattern and magazine size cover for inconsistent aim better than the QQ9 or DR-H.
