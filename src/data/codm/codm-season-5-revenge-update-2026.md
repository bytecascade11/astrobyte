Season 5 dropped on May 27 and it brought more than most players were banking on. A full TV crossover, an entirely new BR experience, a new assault rifle, and one of the more stacked Battle Passes in recent seasons — Revenge is shaping up to be one of the bigger content swings of 2026. ## The Boys Crossover Is the Headliner — and It's Actually Good

The centerpiece of Season 5 is a full collaboration with *The Boys*, Amazon's now-concluded superhero series.

This isn't just a cosmetic bundle slapped onto a Lucky Draw.

Activision went deeper — two dedicated, limited-time game modes built around the show's characters and abilities, with actual gameplay hooks tied to the license.

### Supe'd Up Attack of the Undead (Multiplayer)

This mode drops players into a modified Attack of the Undead. You're assigned as one of three Supes — Homelander, Starlight, or Black Noir — and one player starts as the infected.

Based on early gameplay, each Supe appears to gain a unique power set when they become the final survivor standing. Homelander is said to use aerial laser attacks, Starlight looks to work through electrical crowd control, and Black Noir leans into a stealth-based close-range playstyle.

So far, the ability distribution feels more balanced than you'd expect for a mode like this — it doesn't seem to collapse into "whoever plays Homelander wins." That said, give it a week of live play before drawing firm conclusions on balance.

### Vought Royale (Battle Royale)

The crossover also extends into BR through a special mode running on the Isolated map.

Temp V-inspired power-ups are scattered across the map — pick one up and you get temporary abilities for that round. It's designed to be chaotic, and from what's been reported so far, it delivers on that. Worth loading into at least once for the novelty, especially if Isolated has felt stale lately.

## Armored Royale — The New BR Mode Worth Knowing

Separate from the Vought Royale event, Season 5 also brings Armored Royale as a proper new BR mode.

Squads spawn with access to an armored vehicle that doubles as a respawn anchor.

If the vehicle survives, downed teammates can be brought back through it — which fundamentally changes how squads think about positioning and engagement timing.

Players are finding that vehicle awareness starts to matter as much as ring awareness. Leave your armored unit exposed early and you lose it before the final circles, which cuts off your revival option right when it counts most.

Squads that treat it like a standard BR — ignoring the vehicle until they need it — tend to get punished for it.

It rewards coordination in a way the standard BR playlist often doesn't.

## BAL-27 Assault Rifle — Early Meta Read

The BAL-27 is the new weapon arriving with Season 5, unlockable through the Battle Pass.

Early testing suggests it sits comfortably in the mid-range bracket. Here's how it compares to the current AR pool based on initial impressions — note that attachment tuning and post-launch balance patches may shift these readings:

| Weapon | Damage Profile | Fire Rate | ADS Speed | Expected Range |
|--------|---------------|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| BAL-27 | High | Medium | Fast | Mid-Range |
| AS VAL | Medium | Very High | Very Fast | Close-Mid |
| M13 | Medium | High | Fast | Mid-Range |
| Kilo 141 | Medium | Medium | Medium | Mid-Long |
| DR-H | Very High | Low | Slow | Mid-Long |

At this stage, the BAL-27 plays like a harder-hitting M13 — similar handling feel, more damage per shot, but with one mechanic that sets it apart from anything else in the current AR pool: its fire rate accelerates the longer you hold the trigger. Hold it through a full mag and it's noticeably faster by the end of the burst than when you started. That ramp-up rewards players who commit to a full engagement rather than tapping and resetting — a different rhythm to learn, but one with a real ceiling once it clicks.

Recoil appears manageable stock. Expect the community loadout meta around it to develop over the next week or two as more players get reps in.

## Season 5 Battle Pass Overview

The Revenge Battle Pass runs the standard free/premium split, with a Challenge Pass added as a third track this season.

| Track | Key Rewards |
|-------|------------|
| Free Battle Pass | BAL-27 Assault Rifle, Slide Stabilizers (Red Perk), Operator Skins, Weapon Camos |

The Slide Stabilizers red perk is the standout free unlock this season — worth pushing the free track for alone if you run aggressive movement builds.

## Mythic Kui Ji and The Boys Draws

Season 5 opens with two premium Draw tracks live at launch.

**Kui Ji — Eternal Oathbearer (Mythic Draw)** — The flagship cosmetic drop of the season. Mythic tier means animated elements, custom recall animations, and the full treatment. If you collect Mythics, it's the target. If you just want to play, it's entirely skippable.

**The Boys Draw Series** — Three separate Draws, one per character: Homelander, Starlight, and Black Noir.

Each appears to include a character operator skin and a Legendary Weapon Blueprint themed around that character's visual identity. Homelander's Draw is said to carry the highest pull ceiling of the three — factor that in if you're planning pulls across multiple tracks.

## Seasonal Events Snapshot

Beyond the collab modes, Season 5 runs a full event calendar built around faction progression.

The Boys and Dawn of the Seven are the two rival factions this season.

You pick one side to grind first, clear its reward track, and then unlock the opposing faction's rewards immediately after — no artificial wait gate between them. Players are finding this pacing noticeably smoother than how some past faction events were structured.

That's a quality-of-life call worth appreciating, especially if you've sat through slower event timers in previous seasons.

Other confirmed event content:
- Login rewards active for the full season duration
- Clan missions with bonus cosmetics
- Strongbox rewards
- Lucky Draws featuring upgraded AK117 and VMP blueprints

## FAQ

**When did CODM Season 5 Revenge go live?**
May 27, 2026 at 5 PM PT. For players in Asia, that translates to May 28 at 8 AM GMT+8.

**Is The Boys crossover permanent or limited-time?**
The two game modes — Supe'd Up Attack of the Undead and Vought Royale — are limited-time event modes tied to the season window. Operator skins and weapon blueprints from the Draws are permanent once unlocked.

**How do I unlock the BAL-27?**
Through the Season 5 Battle Pass — free track. No COD Points required to unlock the base weapon.

**What is Armored Royale exactly?**
It's a new BR mode where your squad has access to an armored vehicle that also functions as a respawn anchor. Standard BR rules apply otherwise, but the vehicle layer adds a new strategic dimension to squad play.

**Is the Mythic Kui Ji Draw worth it?**
Depends on your budget and how much the cosmetic matters to you. It's high quality by Mythic standards, but premium Draws don't guarantee low-pull Mythic rates. Battle Pass delivers significantly better value per COD Point if your priority is usable content over collector items.

**Do I need to pick a faction?**
Yes — the main seasonal event is structured around The Boys vs. Dawn of the Seven. You pick one, complete it, then roll into the other faction's track. Both sets of rewards are earnable in a single season.

**What platforms is this update on?**
iOS and Android only. Season 5 is a COD Mobile update — it has no connection to Warzone, Modern Warfare, or the console/PC Call of Duty releases.

**Does 1v1 Duel return this season?**
Yes, the 1v1 Duel event is confirmed to return as part of Season 5's multiplayer event lineup.

**How does Season 5 compare to Season 4 in terms of content volume?**
Early consensus suggests Season 5 is heavier on game mode variety than Season 4 was. Season 4 leaned on weapon balance and loadout tuning — if you were deep in the [Season 4 SMG meta](/codm/best-smg-loadout-codm-season-4-2026), expect a different kind of season this time around. Give the BAL-27 and Armored Royale a genuine run before settling back into your usual setup.
