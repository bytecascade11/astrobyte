Let’s Be Real First

Finding a solid 5G phone under ₦250,000 in Nigeria in 2026 is possible — but you need to understand the trade-offs.

At roughly ₦1,340 per dollar, ₦250,000 sits around $187. This list ranges from about ₦165,000 to ₦252,000 depending on where you shop (Jumia, Slot, or import sellers).

⚠️ Reality Check

Budget 5G phones always compromise somewhere:

- Midrange chips (Dimensity 6300, Unisoc T8300)
- Mostly HD+ displays
- Average cameras

But what you do get:

- Reliable everyday performance
- All-day battery life
- Future-proof 5G support

With MTN, Airtel, and Mafab expanding in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt — buying 5G now makes sense.
