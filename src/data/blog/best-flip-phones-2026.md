## Best Flip Phones in 2026

![Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8 and Motorola Razr Ultra side by side](/assets/posts/best-flip-phones-2026/galaxy-z-flip-8-vs-razr-ultra-hero.jpg)

Flip phones had a quieter year in 2026. Fewer brands showed up, prices edged upward, and Samsung and Motorola ended up shaping most of the choices you can realistically buy.

That makes shopping simpler, but not automatic. The phones differ in battery size, charging speed, thickness, cover-screen behaviour and how long the maker promises to support them. Here is how they compare, and who each one suits.

## Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8: The Safe Pick

Samsung announced the Galaxy Z Flip 8 on July 22, 2026. It costs about $100 more than its predecessor, yet it still starts roughly $300 below Motorola's top model. It is also slimmer when closed: reported folded thickness is 13.1mm, against 15.7mm for the Razr Ultra. That difference should be noticeable when carrying the phone folded in a pocket.

Samsung's strongest argument is longevity. The company promises up to seven years of software updates, while Motorola's 2026 Razr line is reported to get three major Android updates. If you tend to keep a phone for four or five years, that gap deserves more weight than a spec-sheet lead.

![Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8 folded and open](/assets/posts/best-flip-phones-2026/galaxy-z-flip-8-folded-open.jpg)

The compromise is power. Samsung lists a 4,300mAh battery with 25W wired charging. Light and moderate users will likely be comfortable, though heavy users may find the smaller battery less forgiving.

## Motorola Razr Ultra (2026): More Hardware, Higher Price

Motorola confirms a 5,000mAh battery, 68W wired charging, 30W wireless charging and a 4-inch external display for the Razr Ultra. Retail listings also show 512GB of storage and 16GB of RAM as standard. The cover screen can run full apps without opening the phone, which is the main reason to choose a flip over a slab.

It starts at $1,499, and the update window is shorter than Samsung's. If you are unsure how much storage you need before paying for a higher tier, our guide to [Motorola 128GB vs 256GB storage](https://www.revibyte.blog/motorola/motorola-128gb-vs-256gb-storage/) can help you decide.

## Motorola Razr+ (2026): The Middle of the Lineup

The Razr+ sits between the two Razr models, and it deserves a closer look. Motorola lists a 4,500mAh battery with more than 31 hours of claimed battery life, 45W wired charging, a 4-inch cover display and an IP48 rating. It runs on the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3.

On the spec sheet, that puts it between the base Razr and Razr Ultra. It shares the Ultra's 4-inch cover display and carries an IP48 rating, which indicates some dust and water protection, though IP48 does not mean you should take it swimming. Its 45W charging sits below the Ultra's 68W, and the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 is a step below flagship silicon, so it is aimed at everyday performance rather than top-tier speed.

The Razr+ suits buyers who want most of the Razr Ultra experience without the Ultra price. It is also offered in a single colour, which is one less decision to make. Check current pricing in your region, since carrier deals can change the math quickly.

## Motorola Razr (2026): The Lower-Cost Option

At $799 in the U.S., the standard Razr is one of the few current mainstream flip phones available below the $800 mark. That alone earns it a place here.

It gives up some of the performance and extras of the higher models. But if you want the clamshell experience without paying flagship money, it is the most accessible way in.

## Cameras: What the Hardware Actually Means

Motorola gives the Razr Ultra a more ambitious camera setup on paper, with three 50MP cameras, while Samsung takes a different approach with its camera hardware and image processing. A higher megapixel count can help with cropping and detail in good light. It does not guarantee better photos, because sensor size, lens quality and software tuning matter just as much, especially at night.

Flip phones also have less room for large camera hardware than slab phones, since the body has to fold. That is why photography remains a compromise on this form factor. If photos are your top priority, compare a flip against a regular flagship before deciding. ## FAQ

### Which flip phone is best overall in 2026?
For most buyers, the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8 offers the best balance of price, size and software support. Some reviewers prefer the Razr Ultra for its hardware, so the answer depends on what you value.

### What is the cheapest good flip phone in 2026?
The Motorola Razr (2026) starts at $799 in the U.S., making it one of the few current mainstream flip phones below $800.

### Is the Razr Ultra worth the extra money over the Flip 8?
Only if you will use the larger battery, faster charging and extra storage. If you value longer updates and a thinner body, the Flip 8 is the better fit.

### Is the Razr+ a good middle option?
It offers a 4,500mAh battery, 45W charging, a 4-inch cover display and an IP48 rating, according to Motorola. That makes it a sensible step between the base Razr and the Ultra.

### Are flip phones durable enough for daily use?
Durability varies by model. Check each phone's official ingress-protection rating, and remember that a rating like IP48 does not cover every kind of exposure.

### Should I buy a flip phone mainly for photography?
Not as a first choice. They handle everyday photos well, but a regular flagship usually offers more room for camera hardware.
