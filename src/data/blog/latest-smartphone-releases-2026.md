## ✅ CONFIRMED: Phones That Are Already Out

### Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra — February 2026

Samsung announced the S26 series at Galaxy Unpacked on **February 25, 2026**, and it went on sale **March 11**. These aren't rumours — the phone is in people's pockets right now.

The S26 Ultra is the one that does the most. Here's what Samsung actually confirmed and shipped:

- **200MP main camera** with f/1.4 aperture — wider than the S25 Ultra's f/1.7, which makes a real difference in low light
- **Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy** worldwide (no Exynos split on the Ultra — that's a win)
- **6.9-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X display**, up to 2,600 nits
- **5,000mAh battery** with **60W wired charging** — the fastest ever on a Galaxy S series
- **Privacy Display** — this is a real, shipping feature. It's not a screen protector. It's built into the panel itself and narrows your viewing angle on demand so the person beside you at the airport can't read your screen. Samsung calls the underlying tech "Flex Magic Pixel." It works.
- **S Pen included**, aluminum frame, Gorilla Armor 2 protection
- **12GB or 16GB RAM**, storage from 256GB up to 1TB
- Starting price: **$1,299.99** in the US

The camera system also includes a 50MP ultrawide, 10MP 3x telephoto, and a 50MP periscope 5x telephoto with the new ALoP (All Lens On Prism) mechanism. That's a legitimately strong setup, not just a megapixel number flex.

My actual take: the Privacy Display is the most interesting thing Samsung has added in years. Not because it's the most powerful feature — but because it solves a real problem. The camera bump will still make your phone wobble on a table. That hasn't changed. But the core hardware is excellent, and Galaxy AI has genuinely gotten more useful on this generation.

If you're trying to decide between Samsung options at different budgets, I broke down the Samsung side properly in my [best Samsung phones for students](/posts/best-samsung-phones-for-students/) post — there are more affordable S26 models that give you 80% of the Ultra experience.

### Google Pixel 10 Series — August 2025

The Pixel 10 line launched August 20, 2025 (announcement) and August 28 (on sale). The Pro Fold had a later October 9 release. These are all confirmed, shipping phones.

The big engineering story this year was the chip. Google moved Tensor G5 production from Samsung's Exynos fab to **TSMC's 3nm process** — the same node used in Apple's A18 Pro and Snapdragon 8 Elite. The result: cooler running temperatures, better efficiency, and no more thermal throttling complaints that plagued older Pixel models.

What's confirmed across the Pixel 10 series:

- **Tensor G5** (3nm TSMC) — 34% faster CPU, 60% more powerful TPU vs Tensor G4
- **Pixelsnap** — Google's version of MagSafe. First major Android phone in western markets with built-in magnets. Works with Qi2 accessories
- **Android 16 + Material 3 Expressive** out of the box
- **7 years of OS and security updates**
- Pixel 10 base model: 6.3-inch FHD+ OLED, 4,970mAh battery, **48MP main + 13MP ultrawide + 10.8MP telephoto (5x)** — first time a non-Pro Pixel has a telephoto
- Starting at **$799**

The Pixel 10 Pro and Pro XL step up to 50MP main, 48MP ultrawide, 48MP telephoto. The Pro XL hits 45W wired charging.

One thing reviewers have been honest about: the Tensor G5 still underperforms Apple and Qualcomm in raw benchmark numbers. Google's strength has never been chip dominance though — it's the software, the camera pipeline, and Gemini integration. If you're a camera-first buyer, the Pixel 10 Pro still delivers results that beat the spec sheet. I went deep on that in my [best Google Pixel phones 2026](/posts/best-google-pixel-phones-2026/) post.

### Google Pixel 10a — March 2026

The Pixel 10a was announced **February 18, 2026** and went on sale **March 5** — months earlier than any previous A-series Pixel. Google moved the launch window up deliberately, and it worked: the 10a was on shelves before most spring mid-range competition arrived.

Confirmed specs:

- **Tensor G4** — yes, same chip as last year's Pixel 9a. Google kept costs down and maintained the $499 price
- **8GB RAM**, 128GB or 256GB storage
- **48MP main + 13MP ultrawide cameras** — hardware unchanged from Pixel 9a
- **6.3-inch OLED, 120Hz**, 3,000 nits peak (11% brighter than 9a)
- **5,100mAh battery**, Gorilla Glass 7i (upgrade from Gorilla Glass 3 on 9a)
- Completely **flat back design** — no camera bump at all
- No Pixelsnap magnets (that's a 10-series exclusive)
- **$499 starting price** — unchanged from predecessor
- Colors: Berry, Fog, Lavender, Obsidian

The honest truth about the Pixel 10a: it's one of the smallest generational upgrades Google has ever shipped. The chip is a year old. The cameras are identical to the 9a. But Google gave it a better display, better glass, a cleaner design, and it still costs $499 with 7 years of software support and Gemini AI on board. For a first smartphone or a budget upgrade, that value equation is genuinely hard to argue with.

The AI camera tools like Camera Coach and Auto Best Take — which debuted on the Pixel 10 flagship — are on the 10a too. That's where Google earns its money at the budget tier: software features that feel like they belong on a $900 phone.

## FAQ

**Is the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra camera 320MP?**
No — confirmed spec is **200MP** main sensor with f/1.4 aperture. That figure was wrong in earlier coverage. Samsung has not announced a 320MP sensor on any S26 model.

**Is the Privacy Display on the S26 Ultra a real feature?**
Yes. It's built into the display panel at manufacturing level — not a screen protector. When enabled, it significantly narrows the side viewing angle so people next to you can't see your screen. Samsung confirmed this at Galaxy Unpacked 2026.

**Which Pixel phones use Tensor G5?**
The Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold. The Pixel 10a uses Tensor G4.

**Why did Google use last year's chip in the Pixel 10a?**
To keep the price at $499. The trade-off is real — you're not getting the latest silicon — but you get 7 years of software support, Gemini features, and a noticeably better display than the Pixel 9a.

**Is the iPhone 18 officially announced?**
No. As of May 2026, Apple has made no announcement. Everything circulating is leak-based. Expect official news around September 2026.

**Is the Pixel 10 Pro worth buying now or should I wait for Pixel 11?**
If you need a phone today, the Pixel 10 Pro is an excellent choice. If you can wait until October 2026, the Pixel 11's rumored Tensor G6 chip could be a meaningful upgrade — but it's not confirmed yet.

**What's the best budget smartphone available right now in 2026?**
Based on confirmed releases: the **Google Pixel 10a at $499** is the strongest value in this price range. Seven years of updates, Gemini AI, Camera Coach, and a clean Android 16 experience. Nothing Phone (4a) is also worth considering if you want a more design-forward option at a similar price.
