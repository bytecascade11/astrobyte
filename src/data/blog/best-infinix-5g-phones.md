## Overview 

Infinix built its Nigerian reputation on 4G phones that stretched a modest budget further than almost anyone else. 5G is a newer story, and for years the coverage situation made paying extra for it difficult to justify.

That's changed enough in 2026 that it's worth a proper look. MTN Nigeria and Airtel Nigeria have both continued rolling out 5G coverage in major cities, though the pace and reach varies by operator and by state — check MTN's or Airtel's own coverage pages for your specific area before treating 5G as a given. On the hardware side, Infinix now has 5G chipsets running from entry Dimensity silicon all the way up to Snapdragon 7-series hardware. The catch is that "5G" on the spec sheet doesn't tell you much on its own — a Dimensity 7300 Ultimate and a Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 are both 5G-capable, but their performance can differ substantially in games and demanding multitasking.

## 1. Infinix Note 60 Pro 5G — Best Overall

The Note 60 Pro is notable for bringing Qualcomm's Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 to Infinix's current Note lineup, after years of the brand relying almost exclusively on MediaTek chipsets. Snapdragon or not, it's the strongest all-rounder on this list. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 offers a higher performance ceiling than the Dimensity 7400, particularly for GPU-heavy workloads and gaming, and Infinix backs it with a 6.78-inch AMOLED panel running at 144Hz with a 1.5K resolution — sharper than most phones in this bracket.

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| Display | 6.78" AMOLED, 1208×2644, 144Hz, Gorilla Glass 7i |
| Chipset | Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 |
| RAM/Storage | 8GB/256GB or 12GB/256GB |
| Rear Camera | 50MP main (f/1.6) + 8MP ultrawide |
| Battery/Charging | 6,500mAh, 90W wired, 30W wireless |
| Build | IP64, aluminum unibody, vapor chamber cooling |

**Worth knowing:** Infinix rates the IP protection at IP64 — splash resistant, not the IP68/IP69K rating you'll find on some rival mid-rangers at this price. If accidental dunking is a real concern for how you use your phone, that's a genuine trade-off, not a nitpick.

## 3. Infinix GT 20 Pro 5G — Best for Gaming

The GT series is Infinix's gaming-first line, and the Dimensity 8200 Ultimate inside the GT 20 Pro is genuinely a step above the Note series chipsets in raw graphics performance — it's the same silicon tier several 2024–2025 flagship-adjacent phones used. Pair that with a 144Hz AMOLED display and this becomes the pick for anyone whose main use case is MLBB, COD Mobile, or PUBG Mobile rather than camera work.

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| Display | 6.78" AMOLED, 144Hz, 1300 nits peak |
| Chipset | Dimensity 8200 Ultimate |
| RAM/Storage | 8GB or 12GB, 256GB |
| Rear Camera | 108MP main (OIS) + 2MP + 2MP |
| Battery/Charging | 5,000mAh, 45W wired |
| Build | IP54, customizable RGB lighting |

**Worth knowing:** the 108MP main sensor doesn't automatically mean better photos than the 50MP sensors on the Note phones — pixel-binning and processing matter more than raw megapixel count, and Infinix hasn't published independent low-light benchmarks for this exact unit.

## 4. Infinix Note 50x 5G — Best Budget 5G Entry

The 5,500mAh battery and 45W charging carry over from the pricier Note phones, which is the real value story here.

## FAQ

**Does 5G actually work well in Nigeria right now?**
Coverage is real but uneven, and it changes often enough that this article shouldn't be your final source — check MTN's and Airtel's current coverage maps for your specific city before buying on the assumption of 5G access. If you're outside an active coverage zone, a 5G phone will simply fall back to 4G — it won't hurt anything, but you also won't get the speed benefit you paid for until coverage reaches your area.

**Is a 5G Infinix phone worth the price jump over a 4G one?**
It depends on how long you plan to keep the phone. If you're buying for 2–3 years of use, and coverage expands in your area over that period, having 5G hardware already in hand means you won't need to replace the phone just to access the newer network. If you replace phones every year, a 4G Note or Hot series phone may make more financial sense today.

**Which of these five has the best camera?**
On paper, the GT 20 Pro's 108MP sensor and the Note 60 Pro's OIS-equipped 50MP setup are the strongest specs on this list. Neither has been independently lab-tested by us for low-light or video stabilization performance, so treat camera comparisons here as spec-sheet comparisons rather than a hands-on verdict.

**Can I get official Infinix warranty support in Nigeria on all these models?**
Models sold through Infinix's official channels (Jumia's Infinix store, Konga, and authorized Slot outlets) typically carry standard Infinix Nigeria warranty coverage. Third-party marketplace listings and "UK used" imports may not — always confirm warranty terms with the seller before purchase, since this varies by retailer rather than by phone model.

**Do any of these support memory card expansion?**
The Note 50x 5G does, via a shared SIM/microSD slot. The Note 60 5G, Note 60 Pro 5G, GT 20 Pro 5G, and Zero Flip 5G do not have microSD support, so storage tier at purchase is effectively final on those four.
