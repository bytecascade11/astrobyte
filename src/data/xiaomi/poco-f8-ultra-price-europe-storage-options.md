## Overview 

Xiaomi introduced the POCO F8 Ultra in Europe on 26 November 2025 with a limited-time early-bird discount. Once the promotion ended on 10 December, the regular recommended prices applied: €829.90 for the 256GB version and €899.90 for the 512GB model. What shoppers pay today depends on the country and the seller, and the gap between the cheapest and most expensive listings is large.

Here is what the phone costs, which versions exist, and where the extra money makes sense.

![POCO F8 Ultra 256GB and 512GB versions with early-bird and regular prices in Europe](/assets/xiaomi/poco-f8-ultra-storage-options.jpg)

## Only two storage options, and no card slot

Europe gets two versions. RAM is tied to storage, so you cannot mix and match.

| Version | RAM | Storage type | Memory card slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12GB + 256GB | 12GB | UFS 4.1 | No |
| 16GB + 512GB | 16GB | UFS 4.1 | No |

Both come in Black and Denim Blue. Because there is no microSD slot, the capacity you pick at checkout is the capacity you keep.

## Official launch pricing in Europe

| Version | Early bird (26 Nov to 10 Dec 2025) | Regular price |
|---|---|---|
| 12GB + 256GB | €699.90 | €829.90 |
| 16GB + 512GB | €749.90 | €899.90 |

Some early coverage rounded these to €700 and €750, which is why you will see slightly different numbers around the web. The early-bird window saved buyers roughly €130 to €150, and that discount is gone.

## Which storage should you pick?

The real difference between the two versions is storage: double the space on a phone you cannot expand. If you mostly use social apps, messaging, streaming and a handful of games, 256GB is enough. If you record a lot of 4K video, install several large games or keep media offline, 512GB saves you from deleting things every few months.

On price, the maths is simple. When the 512GB model costs about €40 to €80 more, take it. When a shop asks for €150 or more on top, the 256GB version is the better deal. The extra RAM that comes with the larger model is a bonus, but it should not drive the decision.

The rest of the phone does not change between versions: a display marketed as 6.9 inches (about 6.85 inches measured), the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip, a 6500mAh battery and Android 16 with HyperOS 3. ## FAQ

**How much is the POCO F8 Ultra in Europe?**
The early-bird prices were €699.90 for 12GB + 256GB and €749.90 for 16GB + 512GB. The regular prices are €829.90 and €899.90. Retailer prices vary by country and seller.

**What storage options does it come in?**
Two: 12GB RAM with 256GB, and 16GB RAM with 512GB. Both use UFS 4.1 storage.

**Can I add a microSD card?**
No. The phone has no card slot.

**Is the 512GB version worth the extra money?**
Usually yes when the difference is under about €80. If the price gap is much larger, the 256GB model makes more sense.

**Which colours can I buy?**
Black and Denim Blue.

**Why do prices differ so much between European countries?**
Retailers set their own margins, VAT rates differ, and many cheaper listings come from marketplace sellers rather than official stores. Always check the warranty terms.
