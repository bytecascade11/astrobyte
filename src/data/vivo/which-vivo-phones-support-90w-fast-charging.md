## Overview 

vivo has put 90W FlashCharge on a long list of phones, from the V-series up to the X-series flagships. The list is not as long as it looks, though. Lite variants, regional versions and older models can all differ, and the spec sheets rarely make that obvious.

This guide covers the vivo phones with 90W wired charging, the battery each one pairs it with, and the small print that decides whether you will ever see 90W in daily use.

## Regional versions change the numbers

The X300 Pro is the clearest example. vivo's spec page lists 6510mAh for overseas markets outside Europe and 5440mAh for Europe, both at 90W wired and 40W wireless. If you are comparing prices across markets, make sure you are comparing the same version.

Regional battery differences are not limited to the X300 Pro. The regular X300 has a smaller 5360mAh battery in Austria and Hungary, against 6040mAh in the other markets vivo lists.

The X200 Pro's 30W wireless charging is listed by vivo only for Thailand, Malaysia, Hong Kong, Taiwan and India, and it needs vivo's Vertical Wireless Flash Charger 2, which is sold separately. The X300's 40W wireless figure also depends on vivo's wireless charger, so budget for that accessory if wireless matters to you.

## How the V-series fits in

The V50 established 90W in the main V-series lineup, moving up from the 80W charging on the V40. Later models such as the V60 and V70 continued it. Charging still varies between standard and Lite variants, so read the model name carefully rather than assuming the whole family matches.

The X series is where wireless charging shows up: 30W on the X200 Pro and 40W across the X300 range. If battery size matters more to you than wireless speed, the V70 FE's 7000mAh is the largest in the table, and our [roundup of vivo's biggest-battery phones](https://www.revibyte.blog/vivo/vivo-biggest-battery-phone-2026/) covers the wider lineup.

## The small print on "90W"

vivo's wording is careful. 90W FlashCharge means the phone supports up to 90W when paired with vivo's original standard charger, and the actual power adjusts as conditions change. Expect the highest speed early in a charge, tapering as the battery fills.

Two practical points from vivo's pages and reviews:

- **Fast charging may need switching on.** On the V70 FE, vivo says the mode has to be enabled manually the first time, under Settings > Battery > Battery health and charging > Fast charging. Check it before deciding a new phone charges slowly.
- **The charger in the box depends on the market.** GSMArena's V70 review unit, from Thailand, shipped with a 90W charger and a USB-A to USB-C cable. vivo's own X300 and X300 Pro pages list the charger as included outside Europe and not included in Europe, and its Nigeria pages for the V50 and V60 Lite list a charger in the box. Confirm what is included where you are buying.

For a real charge time, the one figure I can point to is GSMArena's listing for the V70 Lite 5G: 100% in 52 minutes. vivo's own numbers come from lab conditions with the phone off and at 1%, so daily top-ups will look different.

## FAQ

### Do all vivo phones support 90W charging?

No. 90W appears on the X-series flagships and on the V50, V60 and V70 families, but the V40 is rated at 80W and entry-level phones charge slower still. Lite and regional variants can also differ from the standard model.

### Can I use any 90W charger with a vivo phone?

vivo ties its 90W claim to its own original standard charger. A third-party adapter may charge the phone, but there is no guarantee it reaches the same speed.

### Does 90W charging wear out the battery faster?

There is no simple answer. Battery wear depends on heat, how often you charge from near-empty to full, the battery's chemistry and its age, and faster charging generates more heat. The EU energy label for the V70, as listed on GSMArena, rates the battery for 900 charge cycles, which is a useful published figure but does not tell you how 90W compares with slower charging.

### Which vivo phone has the biggest battery with 90W?

Among the phones confirmed above, the V70 FE, with 7000mAh. Battery sizes vary by market, so check the variant sold where you live.

### Do the V-series phones have wireless charging?

None of the sources used here list wireless charging for the V50, V60 or V70 families. The wireless speeds above belong to the X series.
