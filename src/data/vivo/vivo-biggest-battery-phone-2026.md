## Overview 

If you only want the number: the largest battery I could confirm on any vivo phone this year is 10,200mAh, shared by the Y600 Pro and the Y600s Pro. Both launched in China. Neither has a confirmed global release, so for most readers the more useful question is which big-battery vivo you can realistically buy. That is where this guide spends most of its time.

Everything below comes from spec pages, launch coverage and review lab data. I have not run battery tests on these phones myself, and the tables say where each figure came from. The information is current as of 26 November 2026, and vivo is launching new models fast enough that it may not stay that way for long.

## The biggest vivo batteries of 2026

This list covers vivo phones launched in 2026 with batteries of 9,020mAh or larger. It is not a list of every vivo phone above 6,000mAh; for that, see our [guide to vivo phones with 6,000mAh or larger batteries](https://www.revibyte.blog/vivo/vivo-phones-6000mah-battery-or-larger/).

A year ago, 6,500mAh felt huge on a vivo. Now it sits in the middle of the pack. Silicon-anode cells, which vivo and others call silicon-carbon, are one of the developments behind the jump because they pack more energy into the same space than older designs. They are part of what lets a phone carry 10,000mAh and still come in under 9mm, though battery chemistry and marketing terms differ between models.

Notice the "typical" and "rated" split on the Y600s Pro. Phone makers advertise the typical capacity, while the rated figure is slightly lower and is what regulators and some reviewers use. A 10,200mAh phone and a 10,000mAh phone are closer than the headline numbers suggest.

vivo is also not alone at this size. Realme's P4 Power is listed at 10,001mAh, so "biggest battery in a vivo" and "biggest battery in a phone" are different claims. This post only makes the first one.

## The one most people will actually consider: V80 Lite 5G

Of the 10,000mAh phones, the V80 Lite 5G is the one that looks most like a normal retail product, with one big warning: there are two versions. The Malaysian model has the 10,000mAh battery and a Dimensity 7300e chip. The version sold in Pakistan has a 7,050mAh battery and a Dimensity 7360-Turbo, and vivo's Bangladesh and Pakistan pages list the smaller cell. Same name, roughly 30 percent less battery.

The 10,000mAh version costs the equivalent of about $349 for the 6GB/128GB model and charges at 44W. vivo says the cell keeps 80 percent of its capacity after 1,400 charge cycles, which is a manufacturer claim and I would treat it as one.

The trade-off is the hardware around the battery. The Malaysian version has a Dimensity 7300e chip and, per Notebookcheck, a 50MP main camera with only a 2MP sensor beside it. The 7,050mAh version also pairs a 50MP main camera with a 2MP bokeh sensor. If you want a phone that survives two days of scrolling, calls and video, that is fine. If you want a camera phone, it is the wrong place to look, and our [vivo V80 camera features explained](https://www.revibyte.blog/vivo/vivo-v80-camera-features-explained/) piece will show you where the V80 family stands on that front.

## Flagships: smaller batteries, better everything else

The X series has not followed the Y series into five-figure territory. vivo says the X300s carries 7,100mAh, the biggest cell yet in an X-series phone. The X300 Ultra sits at 6,600mAh with 100W charging, according to the launch coverage I checked.

So if you are buying a flagship, you are choosing cameras, display and chip over raw capacity. Our [vivo X300 Pro photography and video review](https://www.revibyte.blog/vivo/vivo-x300-pro-photography-video/) covers what you get for giving up several thousand milliamp-hours. One warning from our earlier [guide to vivo phones with 6,000mAh or larger batteries](https://www.revibyte.blog/vivo/vivo-phones-6000mah-battery-or-larger/): some X300 models ship with smaller batteries in Europe than in China, so check the exact regional spec before paying.

## Does 10,000mAh mean 10,000mAh of use?

No. A bigger number is a bigger tank, and the chip, screen and signal strength decide how fast it empties. The clearest example I have is on our own site: the Nothing Phone (4a) Pro has a 5,080mAh battery, and GSMArena's Active Use Score for it is 15h 16m, as we covered in our [Nothing Phone (4a) Pro battery life breakdown](https://www.revibyte.blog/nothing/nothing-phone-4a-pro-battery-life-full-charge/). The 6,500mAh vivo V60 scored almost 16 hours in the same lab. The vivo has roughly 28 percent more capacity and a barely better result, which tells you how much efficiency matters.

For Nigerian buyers, charging speed is half the story. A 10,000mAh phone at 44W takes a long time to fill, and when NEPA decides the evening is over, the first thirty minutes on the charger matter more than the final percentage.

## Before you buy: Nigeria

I could not confirm official Nigerian availability for the Y600 series or the T5 Pro. The V80 Lite 5G is the one to be careful with. vivo Nigeria's official product page lists a 7,050mAh typical battery (6,850mAh rated) and a Dimensity 7360-Turbo chip, with a 50MP Sony main camera, a 2MP bokeh camera and a 32MP front camera. That is not the 10,000mAh Malaysian version with the Dimensity 7300e. Launch coverage of the 7,050mAh model puts charging at 44W. If you are buying locally, use the Nigerian specification, not an international listing. An official product page elsewhere does not mean a phone is stocked here. If you are buying a grey import, ask the seller to show the exact model name and battery capacity in the phone's settings, and check that the version supports the 4G and 5G bands your network uses. A similar model name can hide a smaller battery, and the Y-series has a track record of that.

## FAQ

### Which vivo phone has the biggest battery in 2026?

The vivo Y600 Pro and Y600s Pro, both at 10,200mAh typical capacity. They launched in China, and the Y600s Pro is rated at 10,000mAh.

### Is there a 10,000mAh vivo phone I can buy outside China?

The V80 Lite 5G launched in Malaysia with 10,000mAh, but other markets such as Pakistan got a 7,050mAh version, and Nigerian listings I found show the smaller one. Confirm the exact battery figure before you pay.

### Does a bigger battery mean slower charging?

Not on its own. The V80 Lite 5G pairs 10,000mAh with 44W, while the Y600 Pro pairs 10,200mAh with 90W. Charging speed depends on the charger and the phone's hardware.

### Do the vivo X-series flagships have the biggest batteries?

No. The X300s at 7,100mAh is the biggest in the series, well below the 10,000mAh Y-series phones.

### Is a 10,000mAh battery safe?

Capacity alone does not decide battery safety. Buy from a reputable seller, use a compatible charger and cable, and avoid damaged or poorly made replacement batteries.

### Will vivo release something even bigger?

Probably, given the pace this year, but I would not plan a purchase around a rumour. Check the specs on the day you buy.
