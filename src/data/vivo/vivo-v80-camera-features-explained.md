## Overview 

The vivo V series has traditionally focused on portraits and camera features without entering the highest flagship price bracket. The V80 continues that, but it also pulls in things that used to sit on the pricier X line: a proper periscope telephoto, multiple portrait focal lengths and a cinematic video mode. This guide explains what each camera does, which specifications vivo confirms, and what buyers should know before judging the camera performance.

One thing up front. This is research-based, drawn from vivo's launch specs as reported by several outlets. It is not a review, and none of the image quality claims below come from personal testing.

## The camera system at a glance

The V80 uses a ZEISS-branded triple rear setup plus a front camera with autofocus. Here is how the numbers line up, per the India launch spec sheet.

| Camera | Resolution | Sensor / lens | Notable details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main | 50MP | Sony LYTIA 700V, 1/1.56-inch, f/1.88 | OIS, 84° field of view |
| Telephoto | 50MP | Sony IMX882, 1/1.95-inch, f/2.65 | 3x optical, OIS, up to 100x digital (not optical) |
| Ultrawide | 8MP | 115° field of view, f/2.2 | Fixed focus |
| Front | 50MP | 92° advertised field of view (about 90° after distortion correction), f/2.0 | Autofocus, group selfie mode |

*Confidence: confirmed on vivo India's official specification page. The 1/1.56-inch and 1/1.95-inch sensor sizes come from launch coverage rather than vivo's own table.*

The phone runs on Qualcomm's Snapdragon 7 Gen 4, which handles image processing, so camera speed and AI features depend on it as much as on the sensors.

## The 50MP main camera

A 1/1.56-inch sensor is on the larger side for this price tier, and it is paired with optical stabilization. A bigger sensor gathers more light, which helps after dark. OIS then steadies the lens against your own hand movement, so a slightly longer exposure is less likely to turn soft from camera shake. It does nothing for a moving subject, though. A friend mid-laugh in a dim room can still blur, because the shutter is slow no matter how steady your hands are.

## The 3x periscope telephoto

This is the headline feature. A 50MP telephoto on a 1/1.95-inch sensor is a generous spec for the V series, and the 3x zoom is optical, meaning the lens does the work rather than software cropping the main sensor. The 100x figure is a different thing: it is digital zoom, and at that range detail is largely reconstructed. Use it for reading a distant sign, not for photos you plan to keep.

Where the telephoto earns its place is people. It lets you frame portraits from further away. That extra shooting distance can produce more flattering facial proportions, while the longer framing helps keep attention on the subject.

## The 8MP ultrawide

The ultrawide is the modest member of the trio. Eight megapixels at f/2.2 with a 115° view is enough to fit a whole room, a building front or a big group into frame, which is where it earns its keep on landscapes and crowded gatherings. On paper, its lower resolution and smaller camera module suggest less fine detail and weaker low-light performance than the main camera. Actual image quality will depend on vivo's processing. Treat it as a framing tool rather than a quality one.

## Portrait: five focal lengths and 10x zoom-in

Instead of one generic portrait mode, the V80 offers five: 23mm, 35mm, 50mm, 85mm and 100mm, confirmed in vivo's launch announcement and paired with ZEISS Style Portrait bokeh and colour tuning. Think of it like swapping prime lenses on a camera. 23mm and 35mm suit environmental portraits where the setting matters. 85mm and 100mm are the classic flattering headshot lengths.

vivo also lists a Zoom-In Portrait mode that works up to 10x. The reported benefit is shooting people from further away at events, which is handy at weddings, owambe and church programmes where walking up close is not always practical.

## Video: 4K at 60fps and cinematic styles

The V80 records 4K at 60fps, and vivo notes that 4K is supported only in that 60fps mode. On top of that sits a cinematic video mode that applies background blur and colour grading while you record, with three looks: Modern Movie, Vibrant and Pastel. It is a feature vivo's X series has had, now arriving one tier down.

If you shoot a lot of video, remember it costs battery. The V80 carries a 7,200mAh typical-capacity battery (7,015mAh rated) with 90W wired charging. vivo describes it as the largest in the V series among models launched in India as of October 6, 2026.

## Selfies and AI tools

The 50MP front camera has autofocus and a 92° advertised field of view, with approximately 90° captured after distortion correction. That is wide enough for group selfies without holding the phone at arm's length. Autofocus is the quiet upgrade here, because fixed-focus selfie cameras tend to go soft when you move closer or further.

On the software side, vivo lists an AI Creative Camera with more than 30 preset styles and AI Retouch for expanding backgrounds, erasing objects and repositioning subjects. These tools vary a lot in quality between brands, so judge them on real samples once reviews appear, not on the feature list.

## FAQ

### Does the vivo V80 have a telephoto camera?

Yes. It has a 50MP periscope telephoto with 3x optical zoom and OIS, plus up to 100x digital zoom.

### Can the vivo V80 record 4K video?

Yes, at 4K 60fps. vivo says 4K is supported only in that mode. It also has a cinematic mode with Modern Movie, Vibrant and Pastel colour styles.

### Does the vivo V80 have OIS on the main camera?

Yes, the 50MP main camera has optical image stabilization. The telephoto lens also has OIS.

### Does the vivo V80 have an ultrawide camera?

Yes. vivo India lists an 8MP ultrawide with a 115° field of view and f/2.2 aperture. vivo's India and Philippines listings match, so check the listing for your own market.

### What processor does the vivo V80 use?

The Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 4.

### What battery does the vivo V80 have?

A 7,200mAh typical-capacity battery (7,015mAh rated) with 90W wired charging. vivo calls it the largest in the V series among models launched in India as of October 6, 2026.

### Is the vivo V80 available in Nigeria?

Not confirmed at the time of writing. vivo lists it for India and the Philippines.
