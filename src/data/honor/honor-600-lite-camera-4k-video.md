## HONOR 600 Lite Camera: Does It Record 4K Video? 

No, based on the listings I could find, the HONOR 600 Lite does not record 4K video. Every spec sheet I came across caps video at 1080p, and the ones that state a frame rate say 30fps. That is a strange pairing with a 108MP main sensor, and it is the single detail that will decide whether this phone suits you or not.

This is a research-based guide, not a review. I have not used the phone, and the spec figures come from carrier, retailer and aggregator listings rather than a lab. Where sources disagree, I say so.

## The short answer

The 600 Lite pairs a 108MP main camera with a 5MP ultrawide on the back and a 16MP camera in the punch-hole up front. Retail listings from O2 and several European shops list the video recorder as 1080p at 30fps. MobileMasr's spec summary says the same for both rear and selfie cameras, and flags "no 4K support" as a weakness. Retailer data also lists time-lapse, night mode, portrait mode and panorama, and 91mobiles mentions slow motion.

## What the phone actually records

| Camera | Photo resolution | Maximum listed video resolution | Aperture (as listed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main (wide) | 108MP | Up to 1080p | f/1.75 |
| Ultrawide | 5MP | Up to 1080p (listed for the rear camera system) | f/2.2 |
| Front | 16MP | Up to 1080p | f/2.45 |

On frame rate, O2 and MobileMasr show 30fps, but I have not seen it spelled out for each camera separately, so treat 30fps as a listing figure rather than a per-camera confirmation. Some listings round the main aperture to f/1.8 and the front to f/2.5.

The 108MP main camera is listed at f/1.75, and MobileMasr gives the sensor size as 1/1.67 inch. A megapixel count or a sensor size alone cannot tell you how the camera will do in low light. Lens quality, image processing and exposure all shape the final result. Without independent testing, it is safer to judge this camera by its listed specs than to assume it will beat competing phones.

## How big is the gap between 1080p and 4K?

| Resolution | Pixel dimensions | Total pixels |
|---|---|---|
| 1080p (Full HD) | 1920 x 1080 | about 2.1 million |
| 4K (UHD) | 3840 x 2160 | about 8.3 million |

4K carries four times the pixels. On a 6.6-inch phone screen you will struggle to see the difference when watching a clip on the phone itself. It shows up on a TV, a laptop, or when you crop into a clip during editing.

## Who will not miss 4K

For a lot of buyers, 1080p is enough. A WhatsApp Status, a TikTok, an Instagram Reel or a church-event clip is compressed hard on upload anyway, and most of those platforms rarely show their viewers anything sharper than 1080p. If your phone is mostly for social posts and family videos, the missing 4K will cost you very little. You also save storage. The 600 Lite is listed at 128GB or 256GB with no microSD slot according to 91mobiles, and shorter video files go further. If you are weighing those two storage sizes, our [Motorola 128GB vs 256GB storage guide](https://www.revibyte.blog/motorola/motorola-128gb-vs-256gb-storage/) explains how to judge it, and the maths applies to any phone without expandable storage.

## Who should look elsewhere

If you edit video for clients, post to YouTube, or plan to crop into footage, 4K is the safer starting point. It gives you room to reframe a shot without it going soft. A 1080p file offers very little of that.

If video is your priority at this price, shortlist phones that explicitly list 4K recording on the rear camera. The camera is not the reason to buy this phone. The battery is. HONOR lists a typical capacity of 6520mAh (6320mAh rated) with 45W wired charging, and listings give it IP66 dust and water protection. Long video sessions are easier on a battery this size. ## Can a software update add 4K later?

I found no official announcement that a software update will add 4K recording to the HONOR 600 Lite. Video capabilities depend on the device's hardware, camera software and firmware, so a third-party camera app should not be assumed to unlock higher recording resolutions. If 4K is essential to you, choose a phone that officially supports it rather than buying this one in the hope that a future update adds it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the HONOR 600 Lite record 4K video?

Not according to the listings I found. Rear and front cameras are listed at up to 1080p, with 30fps shown where a frame rate is given.

### Can it record at 60fps?

I did not find a 60fps mode in any listing. Check HONOR's official page before assuming one exists.

### Does the 108MP camera improve video quality?

Not directly. The 108MP figure describes still photos. Video is recorded at a much lower resolution, so the headline megapixel count does not carry over.

### Is 1080p good enough for social media?

For most posts, yes. Platforms compress uploads heavily, and the gap against 4K is hard to spot on a phone screen.

### Is the 600 Lite a good camera phone?

It looks capable for stills on paper, particularly with that 108MP sensor. For video, it is limited by the 1080p cap. If video matters to you, compare it against other options before you buy.
