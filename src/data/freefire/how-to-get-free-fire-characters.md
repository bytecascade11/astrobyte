Characters decide a lot in Free Fire. An ability that heals you, speeds you up, or shields a revive can swing a close fight. The frustrating part is the price tag, and a lot of older guides still point you toward systems that no longer exist.

Here is what works today, and what to skip. This guide was checked against Garena's character-system documentation and 2026 event information. Because Free Fire changes its store and events often, always verify current rewards in-game.

![Free Fire character screen with the Gold purchase option visible](/assets/freefire/free-fire-character-screen-gold-purchase.jpg)

## The main route: buy characters with Gold

Garena reworked the character system in March 2023. Per the patch notes, every character can be bought with Gold, and the character arrives at full skill power. Levels no longer affect skill attributes. That change also retired the old Link system, which is why any guide telling you to "link" a character is out of date.

Gold is an in-game currency you can earn through normal play and various game activities, then spend on characters. That makes this the closest thing to a free path, where "free" means no diamonds and no real money, not no effort. It is also slow if you spend carelessly.

I am deliberately not listing exact prices. Gold costs can differ by character and shift between updates, so open the Character screen and read the number there before you start saving.

A practical order of operations:

1. Decide which ability fits how you already play. The [movement guide](https://www.revibyte.blog/freefire/free-fire-movement-guide/) helps if you are unsure whether you rush or hold positions.
2. Check that character's current Gold cost.
3. Save toward it instead of spending on cosmetics along the way.

## Events with free rewards

Garena runs themed events through the year, and many hand out rewards for completing in-game missions. A recent example is Fire Kickoff, which ran from 5 to 23 June 2026. Players earned tokens through matches and missions and traded progress for a Female Bundle, a football backpack, and a new emote.

Worth stating plainly: that event gave cosmetics, not a character. Events do not always include characters, so treat them as a bonus on top of your Gold plan, not the plan itself. Our [2026 updates breakdown](https://www.revibyte.blog/freefire/free-fire-2026-updates-what-has-changed/) tracks what Garena has changed this season, which is the quickest way to spot event windows.

## Redemption codes and login rewards

Garena runs an official Rewards Redemption site where valid codes can be exchanged for in-game items. Codes can expire, have usage or regional restrictions, and may reward items rather than characters, so don't build your character plan around them. Use the official site only, and never enter a code on a page that asks for your password.

## What to ignore

Sites promising free diamonds or characters in exchange for your player ID are phishing or survey traps. So is anything asking you to share an account or hand over a login. A ranked account is worth far more than any single character.

Once you have your pick unlocked, it only pays off if your aim keeps up. [Our headshots guide](https://www.revibyte.blog/freefire/how-to-get-headshots-in-free-fire/) covers that side.

## FAQ

### Can I get Free Fire characters for free in 2026?

Yes, by earning and saving Gold through normal gameplay and other in-game activities, then spending it on the character you want. It costs time rather than money.

### What happened to the Link system?

Garena introduced it in 2022 and removed it in March 2023 when characters became purchasable with Gold. Guides that still describe it are outdated.

### How much Gold does a character cost?

It varies, and prices change between updates. Check the Character screen in the game for the current figure.

### Do events give free characters?

Sometimes, but not reliably. Fire Kickoff in June 2026, for example, rewarded a bundle, backpack, and emote rather than a character.

### Do free diamond generators work?

No. They exist to collect login details or push surveys. Avoid them.

### Are redemption codes safe?

Only when redeemed on Garena's official Rewards Redemption site. Treat any other page asking for your login as a scam.
