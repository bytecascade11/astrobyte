## Overview 

On paper, the TECNO Camon 50 Ultra and the Camon 50 Pro look almost identical. Same 6.78-inch curved AMOLED, same 144Hz refresh rate, same 50MP main camera with a 3x telephoto behind it. So why does the Ultra cost hundreds of thousands of Naira more?

The short answer: the Ultra is a 5G phone with a much stronger chip, a better selfie and video setup, and a 512GB storage option. The Pro is the cheaper, 4G option that keeps most of the camera hardware.

![TECNO Camon 50 Ultra rear camera close-up](/assets/tecno/tecno-camon-50-ultra-camera-placeholder.jpg)

## Quick answer

Buy the **Ultra** if you game, shoot video, take a lot of selfies, or want 5G. Buy the **Pro** if you mainly want the main and telephoto cameras, a big battery and a curved screen at a lower price. For everyday social media, calls and streaming, the Pro will do the job.

One note on naming: in some markets, such as Indonesia, TECNO also sells a Camon 50 Pro 5G that shares the Ultra's chip. I could not confirm that model in Nigerian retail, so this article compares the Ultra against the standard Camon 50 Pro sold here. If a seller offers you a "Pro 5G", ask for the model number and check it before paying.

## Performance: the chip decides most of it

The Ultra's Dimensity 7400 Ultimate is built on a 4nm process. The Pro's Helio G200 Ultimate uses an older 6nm design. NanoReview's AnTuTu 11 comparison puts the Ultra at roughly 971K against about 569K for the Pro 4G, though its Pro figure rests on a small number of samples.

Treat those numbers as indicative, not guaranteed. Scores shift with software version, temperature, memory configuration and testing conditions. The reliable takeaway is the direction, not the exact figure: the Ultra is clearly faster.

In daily use, that gap shows up when you push the phone: long gaming sessions, 4K editing, or keeping many apps alive in memory. For WhatsApp, TikTok and browsing, both will feel fine. If gaming is your priority, our [Redmi Note 15 vs POCO X7 Pro gaming breakdown](https://www.revibyte.blog/xiaomi/redmi-note-15-vs-poco-x7-pro-gaming/) shows how this chip class compares with rivals.

## Camera: where the Ultra pulls ahead

Rear cameras are close on paper. Both use a 50MP main sensor with OIS, a 50MP 3x telephoto and an 8MP ultrawide. If you mostly shoot with the back cameras, you are not paying the Ultra premium for much.

The differences sit elsewhere:

- **Selfie camera:** 50MP on the Ultra versus 32MP on the Pro in most listings.
- **Video:** The Ultra records 4K at 30fps on the rear camera and supports 4K selfie video. The Pro is listed at 1080p.
- **Processing:** A stronger chip can mean faster shot-to-shot speed and heavier AI processing, though I have not tested that here.

For creators who film Reels or vlogs, the video gap is the difference that matters most.

## Which one should you buy?

**Choose the Camon 50 Ultra if** you shoot a lot of video, take selfies often, play demanding games, or want 5G and extra storage so the phone lasts longer.

**Choose the Camon 50 Pro if** you want the telephoto and main cameras, a curved AMOLED screen and a large battery for less money, and 5G is not a priority yet.

## FAQ

### Is the Camon 50 Ultra worth the extra money over the Camon 50 Pro?

For heavy users, yes. You get 5G, a clearly faster chip, a sharper selfie camera and 4K video. For light users, the Pro covers most daily needs for much less.

### Do the Camon 50 Ultra and Camon 50 Pro have the same rear camera?

On paper, mostly. Both list a 50MP main camera with OIS, a 50MP 3x telephoto and an 8MP ultrawide. The Ultra pulls ahead on video and the front camera.

### Does the Camon 50 Pro support 5G?

The standard Pro sold in Nigeria is a 4G phone. A Pro 5G has appeared in other markets, so check the exact model name before you buy.

### Do these phones support wireless charging?

No. Both are listed with 45W wired charging only.

### Are the Camon 50 Ultra and Pro water resistant?

TECNO lists the Ultra as IP66/IP68/IP69/IP69K and the Pro as IP68/IP69/IP69K. Both ratings are useful against rain, spills and dust, but do not treat it as permission to take the phone swimming regularly.

### Which is better for gaming?

The Ultra, because the Dimensity 7400 Ultimate is the stronger chip. The Pro handles casual games without trouble but has less headroom.

### Where can I buy them safely in Nigeria?

Authorised TECNO stores and verified retailers are the safest route. If you buy on Jiji.ng, ask for the box, IMEI and warranty details, and confirm the exact model and storage in writing.

## Final word

The Camon 50 Ultra is the better phone, but "better" costs a lot more in Nigeria. Decide whether you need 5G, 4K video and gaming power, check today's price, and pay only for what you will actually use.
