## Overview 

Most slim phones make you pay for the thinness at the charger. TECNO's Camon Slim 5G tries to dodge that bargain: it measures 6.39mm at its thickest and still carries a 6,000mAh battery, according to launch coverage and reviews published since its September 2026 announcement.

The number is easy to admire. What matters more is what it does once a Dimensity chip, a 144Hz AMOLED screen and a Nigerian network are all drawing from it.

## What the independent tests show

Several outlets have now put the phone through battery benchmarks. I haven't tested it myself, so the useful move is to read their results side by side rather than pick the flattering one.

| Outlet | PCMark battery test | Video loop | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| [YugaTech](https://www.yugatech.com/gadget-reviews/tecno-camon-slim-5g-review/) | 16h 36m | 20h 53m | Reviewer also reports a full day in normal use |
| [Manila Shaker](https://manilashaker.com/tecno-camon-slim-5g-review/) | 16h 52m | Not reported | |
| [Unbox.ph](https://unbox.ph/gadget/tecno-camon-slim-first-impressions-philippines/) | 14h 45m | Not reported | Says real use gets at least a day |
| [NoypiGeeks](https://www.noypigeeks.com/android/tecno-camon-slim-5g-review/) | 14h 15m | 17h 25m | Suggests nightly charging under heavy use |
| [Gadget Pilipinas](https://www.gadgetpilipinas.net/2026/10/tecno-camon-slim-5g-review/) | Not reported | Not reported | Normal-use observation, not a full discharge: 13h 2m with 20% remaining |

These are separate reviewers' numbers, not a controlled comparison. Brightness, refresh rate, signal and software version all shift a benchmark result, and each outlet sets those up its own way. That probably explains why the PCMark results spread over roughly two and a half hours.

Two things are worth taking from the table. First, the phone lands in respectable territory for a 6,000mAh device, though that is not the same as a two-day phone. Second, a benchmark is not screen-on time. A scripted loop of tasks is steadier than a real day of notifications, camera use and patchy signal, so treat the lowest figures as the safer planning guide and the highest as best case.

PinoyTechnoGuide's reviewer adds a plain-language account of a full day in normal use. One person, one usage pattern.

## What drains it fastest

<div id="waldo-tag-55826"></div>

Three settings matter more than the capacity figure.

**Refresh rate.** The panel can run at 144Hz. Dropping to 60Hz or 90Hz for everyday use is the cheapest way to stretch a charge.

**Signal strength.** A phone hunting for a weak 5G or 4G signal burns noticeably more power than one sitting on a strong tower. If your coverage is patchy, the battery will feel smaller than 6,000mAh suggests.

**Camera and video.** Long recording sessions empty any phone quickly, and this one is no exception.

## Charging: the real concession

This is where TECNO gave something up. The earlier 4G Camon Slim charges at 60W, while this 5G version drops to 45W. In return you get 5G and a bigger cell. Whether that is a fair swap depends on whether you charge overnight (then it barely matters) or top up in a hurry (then it might).

Reviewers disagree on how long a full charge takes. [PinoyTechnoGuide](https://www.pinoytechnoguide.com/?p=31380) reports about one hour and twenty-two minutes, and [GadgetMatch](https://www.gadgetmatch.com/tecno-camon-slim-5g-review-specs-price-philippines/) logged 1h 22m and 1h 23m across two attempts. NoypiGeeks reports a faster 1h 06m, with 92% reached after an hour. That is a gap of roughly a quarter of an hour. Different units, room temperature, background activity and software can all move the result, so treat anything from about an hour to about an hour and twenty as a realistic window.

## Before you buy in Nigeria

Here is a trap worth knowing about. TECNO sells a 4G Camon Slim and a 5G Camon Slim 5G. They look nearly identical, but the 4G version is listed at 5,600mAh with 60W charging, and the 5G version at 6,000mAh with 45W. Check the model name on the box, not just the photo in the ad. The CAMON Slim currently listed on TECNO Nigeria's website is the 4G version, not the CAMON Slim 5G reviewed here.

I could not confirm authorised local stock or an official TECNO Nigeria price for the 5G model at the time of writing. Price-aggregator sites list very different naira figures for the Camon Slim line, so treat any number as unverified until an authorised seller quotes it, and confirm the exact 5G model before paying.

## So, how long does it last?

The TECNO CAMON Slim 5G can comfortably get most moderate users through a day, but its 6,000mAh battery doesn't automatically make it a two-day phone. Independent PCMark tests range from 14 hours 15 minutes to 16 hours 52 minutes, while video-loop results run from about 17 and a half hours to nearly 21. Heavy users should still expect to charge nightly. Its biggest achievement is fitting that capacity into a 6.39mm body, rather than delivering class-leading endurance.

## FAQ

### What is the battery capacity of the TECNO Camon Slim 5G?

6,000mAh, as stated in launch coverage and reviews. The battery is sealed and not user-replaceable.

### How fast does it charge?

It supports 45W wired charging. Reviewers report roughly one hour and six minutes to one hour and twenty-three minutes from empty to full with the bundled charger, depending on the unit and conditions.

### Does it support wireless charging?

No. The specs we checked list no wireless charging.

### How long did it last in tests?

Independent PCMark results ranged from about 14 hours 15 minutes to 16 hours 52 minutes, and video-loop tests ran from 17 hours 25 minutes to about 21 hours. These come from different reviewers using their own settings.

### Will it last a full day?

Reviewers generally say yes for normal use. Heavy use at 144Hz or on a weak signal may mean charging each night.

### Is the 5G model the same as the regular Camon Slim?

No. The regular Camon Slim is a 4G phone with a 5,600mAh battery and 60W charging. The 5G model has a bigger battery and slower charging.

### Is it available in Nigeria?

We could not confirm a Nigerian listing or official naira price for the 5G model. Check with an authorised seller.
