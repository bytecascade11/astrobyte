## Overview 

Ask ten eFootball players who the best manager is and you will get ten answers, and most of them will be right for the squad that person happens to own. That is the honest starting point for 2027. The season brought double Link-Up managers, which makes the old habit of simply picking the biggest number even less reliable than before.

So this guide does not crown one name and walk away. It ranks the strongest cards by what they actually do, shows which of the six Team Playstyles each one suits, and tells you when a cheaper option makes more sense. If you are rebuilding around a new setup, sort your defence first with our guides to the [best defenders](https://www.revibyte.blog/efootball/best-defenders-efootball-2026/) and [best CB pairings](https://www.revibyte.blog/efootball/best-cb-pairings-efootball-mobile-2026/), since the manager has to fit the players behind him.

## What makes a manager "best" in 2027

Two things matter more than anything else on the card: the Team Playstyle proficiency and the Link-Up Plays.

Proficiency is the rating next to each playstyle (Possession Game, Quick Counter, Long Ball Counter, Out Wide, Long Ball, Overload). A 90 in the playstyle you actually play is worth far more than a 90 in one you never use. Link-Up Plays are the extra layer. Each has its own Centre Piece and Key Man requirement, and it only switches on when your starting eleven has the right player styles in the right positions.

Konami introduced managers with two Link-Up Plays in [the v6.0.0 update](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en/page/v6/versioninfo_v6-00), alongside the new Overload playstyle. Not every card has the same number. Klopp (current card), Conte, Arteta, Wenger and Chivu carry two Link-Up Plays, while Tuchel, Deschamps, Montella and Koeman carry one each. A second Link-Up adds another tactical option, but both combinations must meet their activation requirements at the same time. If they do not, you are paying for an ability that sits unused.

## Best manager for each playstyle

**Long Ball Counter: Arteta.** His 90 edges Deschamps and Koeman, who both sit at 89. Arteta is also the only card in this guide that pairs a 90 in Long Ball Counter with a 90 in Overload, and he brings two Link-Ups. The catch is the price of entry: he comes in the 1,500-coin Arsenal FC 26-27 Club Pack, so check the in-game timer for how long it stays on sale. If you cannot get him, Deschamps suits squads with a Creative Playmaker at AMF feeding the striker, and Koeman prefers an Orchestrator in central midfield supplying a Prolific Winger.

**Quick Counter: Klopp or Conte.** Both sit at 90 and both run two Link-Ups. Klopp is the pick if you want a bridge into Overload. Conte is better when your wingers do most of the damage, thanks to his 89 in Out Wide.

**Possession Game: Tuchel or Wenger.** Both list 90, and both keep 89 in Quick Counter, so switching style mid-season will not strand you. Tuchel's Link-Up uses an Orchestrator at DMF and a Goal Poacher at CF, a pairing many 4-3-3 and 4-2-1-3 squads already have. Wenger is the newer of the two and arrives through a 750-coin Manager Pack; his Link-Up requirements are covered below.

**Overload: Arteta, then Klopp.** Overload is the newest playstyle, and options are thin. Arteta's 90 is the highest we found. Klopp's current card is the alternative at 83 while keeping 90 in Quick Counter.

**Out Wide: Chivu, Conte, Montella or Koeman.** Chivu leads at 90, with Conte, Montella and Koeman at 89. Chivu's low Possession Game (51) makes him a poor fit for patient short passing, so he suits wing-based attacks. Pick by how you attack, not by the name on the card.

**Long Ball: Chivu.** He lists 90 here as well, which makes him the clearest Long Ball option in this guide. Few managers specialise in this style, so check the exact card before buying.

## What the double Link-Up managers actually ask for

A Link-Up pairs a Centre Piece with a Key Man, each with a required playing style and position. These are the requirements I could confirm for the newest double Link-Up cards. Check the card itself for Klopp, Conte and Arteta, whose exact requirements I could not verify.

| Manager | Link-Up | Centre Piece and Key Man |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wenger | Breakthrough Pass B | Box-to-Box CMF and Goal Poacher CF |
| Wenger | Shadow Link C | Hole Player AMF and Deep-Lying Forward CF |
| Chivu | Aggressive Centring A | Cross Specialist at LMF or RMF, with a Fox in the Box CF |
| Chivu | 1-2 Cut-in A | Creative Playmaker at LMF or RMF, with a Fox in the Box CF |

*Sources: [Sportsdunia's Wenger report](https://www.sportsdunia.com/esports/efootball-adds-new-arsene-wenger-manager-pack) and [GameMarket's Chivu breakdown](https://gamemarket.gg/news/efootball/efootball-inter-milan-26-27-pack-is-chivu-worth-1-500-coins).*

Notice how different the two cards are. Wenger's two Link-Ups both sit through the middle, so a Box-to-Box midfielder and a Deep-Lying Forward can serve both. Chivu's both depend on the same Fox in the Box striker, which is convenient if you own one and a dead end if you do not.

## How to choose without wasting coins

Build your starting eleven first. Then look at managers.

Check the proficiency in your main playstyle, confirm the formation fits, and only then read the Link-Up conditions against the players you already trust. If two managers are within a single point of each other, take the one whose Link-Ups you can activate today. That will usually help you more than forcing an expensive signing into the team just to light up a label.

The same logic applies to the rest of your squad. ## Frequently asked questions

**Who is the best manager in eFootball 2027?**
There is no single answer. Arteta leads for Long Ball Counter and Overload, Klopp and Conte for Quick Counter, Tuchel and Wenger for Possession, and Chivu for Out Wide.

**Who is the best Overload manager?**
Mikel Arteta, with a reported 90 in Overload. Klopp's current card is the alternative at 83. Lampard was reported as a free Overload option earlier in the season, so check whether it is still available.

**What is a double Link-Up manager?**
A manager card with two separate Link-Up plays. Each needs its own Centre Piece and Key Man in the right positions before it activates.

**Is Antonio Conte worth it in eFootball 2027?**
Yes, if you play Quick Counter or Out Wide and your squad can meet both Link-Up conditions. Overload is not his strength.

**Does the highest rating always win?**
No. Formation, player roles and Link-Up conditions often matter more than a one-point gap in proficiency.

**Will these rankings change?**
They will. New cards, limited-time packs and balance updates keep moving the picture, so confirm the version number and the exact card before you spend coins.
