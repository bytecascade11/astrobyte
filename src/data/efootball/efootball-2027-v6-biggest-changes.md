## Overview 

Konami's annual season transition landed on August 13, 2026, and it came with more than the usual squad refresh. The v6.0.0 seasonal update moves the game into the 2026/27 season — widely referred to as eFootball 2027 — and it's one of the more substantial seasonal updates the series has received recently: a new Team Playstyle, a formation system that changes shape on its own, a redesigned way to score off crosses, and a mode that lets players host their own tournaments.  
  
**Quick answer:** eFootball 2027 v6.0.0 is the 2026/27 seasonal update, released August 13, 2026. It introduces Fluid Formation, the new Overload Team Playstyle, separate attacking and defensive Playing Styles, mid-air Stunning Shots via the new Dynamic Volley command, Custom Tournaments, new Daily Game mini-games, the Moroccan League, and a long list of dribbling, heading, and goalkeeper adjustments.  
  
I've gone through Konami's official v6.0.0 patch notes to pull out what actually matters, rather than repeating the trailer beat for beat. As always on this site: developer claims are labeled as such, and anything still being tested community-side is flagged rather than presented as confirmed fact.

## The 10 biggest changes  
  
**1. Fluid Formation.** [Konami confirms](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en/page/v6/versioninfo_v6-00) Game Plans now shift automatically based on whether your team has the ball — an attacking shape built around build-up play, a defensive one built around a compact block, without you pausing to switch manually. Auto-Pick has also been expanded to set up both formations for you.  
  
**2. Overload — a new Team Playstyle.** Confirmed by Konami: Overload has been added as a new Team Playstyle that concentrates players on the side of the ball to create numerical superiority — easier short passing and possession in attack, a compact block that closes down ball carriers in defense. (Some outlets describe it as the "sixth" Team Playstyle overall; Konami's own notes don't number it, so that count is secondhand rather than something the official page states directly.) What's still community observation rather than confirmed fact: early guides suggest the far side of the pitch can be left exposed if you don't keep a designated outlet there, since Overload pulls bodies toward the ball.  
  
**3. Attacking and defensive Playing Styles.** Individual players can now carry separate Playing Styles for when their team has the ball and when it doesn't — Konami's own example is a player who's a Goal Poacher in attack and a Front Line Pressure presence in defense. It's a companion piece to Fluid Formation rather than a standalone feature.  
  
**4. Dynamic Volley / Stunning Shot from mid-air.** You can now trigger a Stunning Shot while the ball is still in the air, regardless of height, off a cross, a lofted pass, or a loose second ball. Konami's notes specifically call out cases where players used to attempt a weak headed shot off a low lofted pass — this is aimed at replacing that with a proper volley.  
  
**5. Custom Tournaments.** Players can host their own knockout tournaments, invite friends or the wider community, and set custom rules — for example, restricting eligible players by league, club, or region. Konami's official page confirms a cap of up to 8 players per tournament, and the host can spectate other participants' matches in real time, switching cameras to watch from different angles.  
  
**6. Two Link-Up Plays for select managers.** Certain managers now arrive with two Link-Up Plays instead of one. Konami confirms the feature exists and that Auto-Pick can now build a lineup that satisfies both sets of conditions, but hasn't detailed exactly how two simultaneous Link-Up Plays interact mechanically in a live match.  
  
**7. New mini-games added to the Daily Game.** Some outlets have referred to this as "Daily Super Play," but Konami's own patch notes describe it more simply: new mini-games layered onto the existing Daily Game mode, where you control a randomly selected Special Player List player and try to time a wondergoal.  
  
**8. Dribbling, heading, and goalkeeper adjustments.** The patch notes list a long set of gameplay tuning changes: new dribbling motions and more stable turning, a slightly lower Dash Dribble top speed, more consistent use of a player's stronger foot, revised heading trajectories, and expanded goalkeeper animations for saves, blocks, and 1v1 situations.  
  
**9. Position Token fix.** A long-requested change is here: a player's already-registered position no longer shows up as a learnable option when you use a Position Token on them, so tokens are less likely to go to waste.  
  
**10. New league content and season-transition changes.** Two separate things happen at once with this update, and it's worth keeping them apart. Content additions: the Moroccan League joins the roster, alongside new partner clubs and updated licenses, kits, and player data for the 2026/27 season. Season-transition/account-state changes: Courtesy Ratings reset to A for everyone, Match History clears, and Balance Sheet data carries over — worth knowing before you check your stats after the update.  
  
## What changed, at a glance  
  
| Area | Before v6.0.0 | After v6.0.0 |  
|---|---|---|  
| Formation | Single static shape all match | Fluid Formation: separate attack/defense shapes |  
| Team Playstyles | 5 options | 6, adding Overload |  
| Player Playing Styles | One style regardless of phase | Separate attacking and defensive styles |  
| Aerial finishing | Limited mid-air shot options | Stunning Shot usable on volleys from crosses/second balls |

## What matters most for mobile players  
  
Not every change on this list will land the same way for everyone. For ReviByte's mostly mobile audience, the changes worth prioritizing are Fluid Formation, Overload, the mid-air Stunning Shot, and the updated goalkeeper and dribbling behavior — these directly change how matches play, regardless of platform. Custom Tournaments is probably the biggest social addition if you play with a regular squad of friends, since it removes the need to coordinate fixtures outside the app. The formation system is the one most likely to change how your matches actually feel match to match, so it's worth spending your first few sessions in Training or Friend Match getting a feel for how your team behaves in each phase before taking it into ranked play.  
  
## FAQ  
  
**Is eFootball 2027 a new game or an update?**  
It's the seasonal update to the existing live-service title, versioned v6.0.0. Konami uses the "2027" label the same way it used "2026" and "2023" before — as a season branding, not a separate paid release.  
  
**Does v6.0.0 work on mobile?**  
Yes. Konami's notes list the update across PlayStation, Xbox, Steam, Windows, iOS, and Android, though a few menus present slightly differently on mobile versus console/PC.  
  
**Will my Dream Team progress carry over?**  
Balance Sheet data carries over, but Courtesy Ratings reset to A and Match History is cleared as part of the season transition — this is standard for eFootball's yearly updates, not something unique to v6.0.0.  
  
**Is Master League affected in this update?**  
No confirmed Master League addition is part of v6.0.0. Konami's official update notes don't list a new Master League feature or major Master League change.  
  
**What's the safest formation to try with Fluid Formation right now?**  
Early community testing leans toward a conservative base like 4-2-3-1 defensively that opens into a more attacking shape in possession, but this is still early-season theorycrafting rather than a settled meta — treat it as a starting point, not a guarantee.
