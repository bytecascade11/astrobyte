## Overview 

If you're building a squad around one striker who can decide matches on their own, the Centre Forward slot is where that decision actually gets made. eFootball 2027's v6.0.0 update didn't just refresh ratings — it gave individual players separate attacking and defensive Playing Styles for the first time. That's not a rumor: pulling Cristiano Ronaldo's Standard CF card directly from pesdb.net's database shows exactly that split in practice — Attacking Playing Style "Goal Poacher," Defensive Playing Style "Basic," listed as two separate fields on the card itself. That mechanic changes what "best CF" means this cycle, since two similarly-rated strikers can now behave very differently depending on which phase of play you're watching.

**Ranking methodology:** Rankings below combine each card's current Overall Rating, its confirmed Playing Style where available, and how well that profile fits eFootball 2027's dominant team tactics — Quick Counter, Long Ball Counter, and the newer Overload playstyle. OVR is the starting filter, not the deciding factor. Two labels appear throughout this guide: **[Database fact]** for anything pulled directly from a live card entry, and **[Formation take]** for this site's editorial read on how that card plays in a specific system. The two are never blended into one unlabeled sentence.

**Sourcing note:** eFootballLAB's live eFootball 2027 player database was used as the primary source for Overall Ratings, cross-checked against pesdb.net's eFootball 2027 card export for individual player pages and Playing Style data. Exact card type and Playing Style were confirmed by opening the individual player page for Mbappé, Ronaldo, and Sesko; for the remaining seven entries, only their OVR and position were confirmed against eFootballLAB's live top-cards list, and that limit is stated directly in the table rather than papered over.

## Breaking Down the Top Picks

**Kylian Mbappé** stays the benchmark CF this cycle. **[Database fact]** His current best CF card is listed as card type "New" on eFootballLAB, rated 96 OVR with a confirmed Goal Poacher Attacking Playing Style on that specific card page. **[Formation take]** In practice, that profile suits systems that want the CF attacking space behind the defensive line rather than constantly dropping deep — pair him with a Quick Counter manager and he punishes a defense before it resets, which is the specific reason he tops a 4-3-3 built around transitions rather than a possession-first shape.

**Bryan Mbeumo.** **[Database fact]** Confirmed OVR is 95, and a matching eFHUB entry at the same rating lists a Goal Poacher Attacking Playing Style; this guide did not open his individual eFootballLAB page to confirm the exact card type. **[Formation take]** If that style holds, he isn't a different playstyle wearing a lower rating than Mbappé, he's a near-identical positioning instinct — which makes him a strong alternative for players who want a Goal Poacher profile without using Mbappé, particularly as a second CF option in a 4-2-3-1.

**Cristiano Ronaldo.** **[Database fact]** His current best CF card is the Trending "POTD International Cup Day 13" version — 95 OVR, Goal Poacher attacking style, Basic defensive style, confirmed directly on pesdb.net's player page for that specific card. His Standard card, by contrast, sits at just 81 OVR — a reminder of how wide the gap between a player's cards can be. **[Formation take]** His 95 OVR rating puts him in the same elite tier as Mbeumo, but the two cards can still feel different in practice, since attributes, skills, and player-specific movement patterns matter beyond a shared OVR and playstyle label — this guide hasn't done an attribute-by-attribute comparison of the two. The clearer practical difference is availability: Featured-pack cards like this one aren't always guaranteed to stay obtainable once the pack rotates out.

**Benjamin Sesko.** **[Database fact]** Confirmed OVR is 94, and a matching eFHUB entry lists a Goal Poacher Attacking Playing Style, though this guide did not confirm the exact card type on his individual eFootballLAB page. **[Formation take]** His size and physical profile make him the pick when your midfield struggles to break lines with through balls — he fits a Long Ball Counter setup where winning the first contact matters more than pace. Aubameyang, Lautaro Martínez**, and **Omar Marmoush** all sit in the 83–87 OVR band **[Database fact]**, with no confirmed Playing Style or card type from the sources checked. **[Formation take]** By known role: Costa and Suárez suit Overload's tight combination play near the box, Eto'o's reputation fits a Fluid Formation CF exploiting space behind an advancing full-back, Aubameyang offers pace for a Quick Counter attack, Lautaro is a balanced pressing forward for Overload, and Marmoush offers a similar pace-plus-link profile as a versatile alternative.

## Which Formation Suits Your CF?

Your CF choice should follow your formation, not the other way around. **[Formation take]** throughout this section:

- **4-3-3 (Quick Counter):** wants a Goal Poacher with pace who can attack the channel left by an advancing full-back — Mbappé, Mbeumo, or Aubameyang all fit, because the whole system depends on beating the defensive line before it resets.
- **4-2-3-1 / Long Ball Counter:** rewards a target CF who can win the first header or hold up play for a late-arriving AMF — Sesko is the clearest fit, since his size lets him compete in the air and buy time for support to arrive.
- **Overload:** needs a CF who can receive under pressure near the edge of the box and lay off short combinations rather than just running in behind — Costa, Suárez, and Lautaro suit this better than a pure speedster, because Overload's numerical advantage only works if the CF can actually keep the ball once it arrives.
- **4-2-2-2:** benefits from a CF who can drop toward midfield to link play while a strike partner attacks the space that opens up behind them — a deep-dropping profile matters more here than in a static single-striker system, since the whole shape depends on that rotation.

## FAQ

**Who is the best CF in eFootball 2027 right now?**
By confirmed OVR, Kylian Mbappé leads at 96, with Bryan Mbeumo and a Trending Cristiano Ronaldo card tied at 95 — all three verified against eFootballLAB's and pesdb.net's eFootball 2027 databases.

**Is a high-rated CF always the right pick?**
No. Overall Rating is a useful first filter, but Position Proficiency, attributes, skills, and — since v6.0.0 — separate attacking and defensive Playing Styles all affect how a CF performs in your specific formation. A well-fitted 86 OVR CF can outperform a poorly-fitted 94 OVR one.

**Which Cristiano Ronaldo CF card is the best one in eFootball 2027?**
His Trending "POTD International Cup Day 13" card is the highest-rated at 95 OVR with a Goal Poacher attacking style, confirmed on pesdb.net. His Standard card sits far lower at 81 OVR, so check exactly which version you own before training points go into him.

**What's the best budget CF for a new Dream Team squad?**
P.-E. Aubameyang and Lautaro Martínez both sit at a confirmed 83 OVR and can serve as lower-rated alternatives while you save resources for higher-tier cards.

**Does the best CF change with each eFootball update?**
Yes — ratings, Playing Styles, and Position Proficiency are all adjusted by Konami with major version updates like v6.0.0, so it's worth rechecking a CF's exact in-game stats before locking in a squad for competitive play.
