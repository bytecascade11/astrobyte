## Overview 

At a glance, the Razr 60 and the Razr 60 Ultra look like the same phone in two sizes. Same clamshell shape, same cover screen idea, same hinge-first personality. The gap shows up once you look at what is inside, and at the price tag, which at launch was roughly double.

So the real question is not "which one is better". The Ultra wins most spec rows, as you would expect. The better question is whether those wins matter for how you use a phone.

## Before you buy: these are 2025 phones

Motorola has released newer Razr models since these two launched in 2025, so the Razr 60 series may be discounted or harder to find new. A lower price can change the math considerably, so check current listings before you decide.

## Quick answer

Pick the **Razr 60** if you want a stylish flip phone for calls, social apps, streaming and everyday photos, and you would rather keep the money. Pick the **Razr 60 Ultra** if you want flagship-class speed, the faster charging, and a cover screen you can lean on for more than notifications.

## Performance: the biggest gap

This is where the money goes. The Razr 60 uses a mid-range Dimensity chip, and for chat apps, video, browsing and casual games it should feel perfectly fine. The Ultra uses Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite with double the RAM, which puts it in a different tier for heavy multitasking, long gaming sessions and editing video on the phone.

If your phone time is mostly WhatsApp, TikTok and YouTube, you may never push the Razr 60 hard enough to miss the extra power. For demanding games, the Ultra has considerably more processing power, although heat management and the compact foldable design can affect sustained performance on either phone.

## Displays: bigger and smoother on the Ultra

Both phones fold into a pocketable square, and both have a pOLED inner screen with a crease you will notice at certain angles and forget about at others. The Ultra's panel is slightly larger and refreshes faster, up to 165Hz against 120Hz on the Razr 60.

The cover screen is the more interesting difference. At 4 inches, the Ultra's outer display gives you more room to reply to messages, use the camera viewfinder and run widgets without opening the phone. The Razr 60's 3.6-inch screen does the same jobs, just with less space to work in.

## Cameras: the Ultra upgrades the ultra-wide and selfie cameras

Both phones share a 50MP main camera, so for daylight photos the two should land closer than the price gap suggests. The differences sit around it. The Ultra upgrades the ultra-wide camera from 13MP to 50MP and the front camera from 32MP to 50MP. That gives it higher-resolution hardware, but it does not automatically mean better photos. Sensor size, lens quality, image processing and lighting all shape the final result, so real-world comparisons tell you more than megapixel counts.

Real endurance depends on screen brightness, how often you use the cover screen, gaming, network conditions and software optimisation. ## FAQ

### What is the main difference between the Razr 60 and Razr 60 Ultra?

Performance and the cover screen. The Ultra uses a Snapdragon 8 Elite chip with 16GB of RAM and a larger 4-inch cover display, while the Razr 60 uses a mid-range Dimensity 7400X with a 3.6-inch cover screen.

### Is the Razr 60 Ultra worth the extra money?

It depends on how you use your phone. For gaming, heavy multitasking, faster charging and better ultra-wide and selfie cameras, the Ultra offers clear upgrades. For everyday social, streaming and calling use, the Razr 60 may be all you need.

### Do both phones support wireless charging?

Yes. The Razr 60 supports 15W wireless charging and the Razr 60 Ultra supports 30W, based on the launch specifications.

### Which phone has the better camera?

The two share a 50MP main camera, but the Ultra has a higher-resolution ultra-wide (50MP versus 13MP) and selfie camera (50MP versus 32MP). Real-world results also depend on Motorola's software processing.

### Are the Razr 60 and Razr 60 Ultra water resistant?

Both carry an IP48 rating. The 8 covers water, but the 4 means dust protection only extends to solid objects larger than 1mm, so fine dust and sand can still get in. That matters on a hinged phone, so keep them away from sand and dusty environments.

### Does either phone have a microSD slot?

No. Neither phone supports expandable storage, so pick the storage size you will need from the start.

### Which one is lighter?

The Razr 60 is slightly lighter at about 188g, compared with about 199g for the Ultra.
